2AM (band) | 2AM ( band )
 2AM ( ) is a South Korean boy group , that consisted of Jo Kwon , Lee Chang-min , Lim Seul-ong and Jeong Jinwoon . It was one of the two subgroups split from the eleven-member boy band One Day , the other being 2PM . They officially debuted on July 11 , 2008 , on KBS Music Bank , performing the song This Song . They won their first Mutizen at Inkigayo on February 7 , 2010 , with Cant Let You Go Even If I Die .

Garnidelia | History
 The duo first collaborated on the song Color , which was used as the opening theme to the 2010 anime television series Freezing . The name of their unit came from the French phrase Le Palais Garnier de Maria ( Marias Opera ) , and the asteroid , which was discovered in 1978 , the year of Tokus birth .

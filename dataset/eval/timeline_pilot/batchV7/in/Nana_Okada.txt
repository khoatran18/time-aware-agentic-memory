Nana_Okada | Personal life
 Okada lives in Kanagawa Prefecture , but was born in Neyagawa in Osaka Prefecture and lived there until she was three years old . She has two older brothers and a younger sister . Her eldest brother is a medical professional and often gives her dietary advice . Her sister , Rina , occasionally accompanies her in live streaming sessions and public appearances , notably at the 2016 AKB48 Janken Tournament where they cosplayed as characters from the anime series . The family owns two Toy Poodles .

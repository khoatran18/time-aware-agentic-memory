Shelter_Island_Hong_Kong | Shelter Island ( Hong Kong )
 Shelter Island , known in Cantonese as Ngau Mei Chau ( ) is an island located in the water body Port Shelter ( Ngau Mei Hoi ; literally Cow Tail Sea ) , in the Sai Kung District , the New Territories , Hong Kong S.A.R. , China .

According to the historical document of the District Office South , the island was uninhabited when it was part of the Port Shelter Firing Range . The firing range was closed in the 1970s . In 2011 , a mortar shell was discovered on a beach of the island . Administratively , the District Office South was replaced by the Sai Kung District Office as well as other District Offices after the World War II . Shelter_Island_Hong_Kong | Biodiversity
 24 species of vascular plant were discovered on the island . Corals also appears in the water surrounding the island . However , survey discovered that the coverage rate is decreasing , from 50.6% to 31.2% , according to the interpretation of Ming Pao on the survey summary . According to another research , Shelter Island has a high number of Drupella rugosa and Cronia margariticola , which preying coral species Pavona decussata and Platygyra sinensis in the location . That research reported that the coverage of hard coral at Shelter Island , was 50.2% at that time .

A marine park that covers Sharp Island , Tai Chau , Shelter Island and surrounding water was proposed by the Country and Marine Parks Board , a consultative body for the government in 2014 . However , it was not materialized . In 2018 , World Wide Fund for Nature also proposed to establish the Port Shelter Marine Protected Area , which also includes the Shelter Island .

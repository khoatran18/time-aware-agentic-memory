Western_Military_District_Sweden | Western Military District ( Sweden )
 Western Military District ( , Milo V ) , originally III Military District ( ) was a Swedish military district , a command of the Swedish Armed Forces that had operational control over Western Sweden , for most time of its existence corresponding to the area covered by the counties of Gothenburg and Bohus , Älvsborg , Skaraborg ( all three now joined as Västra Götaland County ) and Halland . The headquarters of Milo V were located in Skövde .
=== CHUNK /wiki/Western_Military_District_(Sweden)#1 ===
Western_Military_District_Sweden | History
Milo V was created in 1966 along with five other military districts as part of a reorganisation of the administrative divisions of the Swedish Armed Forces . It can be seen as the successor of III Military District ( III . militärområdet ) created in 1942 , but that did not have the same tasks as Milo V . The military district consisted of the land covered by the above-mentioned counties .

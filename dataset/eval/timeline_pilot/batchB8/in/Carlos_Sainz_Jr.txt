Carlos_Sainz_Jr | Carlos Sainz Jr .
 Carlos Sainz Vázquez de Castro , otherwise known as Carlos Sainz Jr . or simply Carlos Sainz , ( born 1 September 1994 ) is a Spanish racing driver competing in Formula One for Scuderia Ferrari . He is the son of Carlos Sainz , a double World Rally Champion , and the nephew of rally driver . In 2012 Sainz raced in the British and European Formula 3 championships for Carlin . He raced for DAMS in the 2014 Formula Renault 3.5 season , winning the championship before moving to F1 with Toro Rosso .

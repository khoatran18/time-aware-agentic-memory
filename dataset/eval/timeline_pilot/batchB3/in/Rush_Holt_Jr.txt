Rush_Holt_Jr | Rush Holt Jr .
 Rush Dew Holt Jr . ( born October 15 , 1948 ) is an American scientist and politician who served as the U.S . Representative for from 1999 to 2015 . He is a member of the Democratic Party and son of former West Virginia U.S . Senator Rush D . Holt Sr . He worked as a professor of public policy and physics , and during his tenure in Congress he was one of two physicists and the only Quaker there .
=== CHUNK /wiki/Rush_Holt_Jr.#1 ===

Holt sought the Democratic nomination for U.S . Senate in the 2013 special primary election to fill the seat of U.S . Senator Frank Lautenberg , who died in office on June 3 , 2013 . He lost the nomination to Newark Mayor Cory Booker . Holt announced on February 18 , 2014 that he would not seek re-election in 2014 .

Rush_Holt_Jr | Rush Holt Jr .
Holt was born in Weston , West Virginia , to Rush Holt Sr . ( 1905–1955 ) , who served as a United States Senator from West Virginia ( 1935–1941 ) , and his wife , Helen Froelich Holt ( 1913–2015 ) , the first woman to be appointed Secretary of State of West Virginia ( 1957–1959 ) . Holt Sr . was the youngest person ever to be popularly elected to the U.S . Senate , at age 29 . He died of cancer when Rush was six years old .
=== CHUNK /wiki/Rush_Holt_Jr.#4 ===
Rush_Holt_Jr | Rush Holt Jr .
 Holt graduated from the Landon School in Bethesda , Maryland , in 1966 , then later graduated Phi Beta Kappa with a BS in physics from Carleton College in 1970 , and later received his MS and PhD degrees in physics from New York University in 1981 . The title of his doctoral dissertation was Calcium absorption lines and solar activity : a systematic program of observations .

Lambertville Mayor David DelVecchio won the primary with 45% of the vote and Carl Mayer finished second with 31% of the vote .

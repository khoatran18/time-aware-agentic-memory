David Noel Livingstone ( born 15 March 1953 ) is a Northern Ireland-born geographer , historian , and academic . He is Professor of Geography and Intellectual History at Queens University Belfast .

[#1] David N. Livingstone | Personal background
David Livingstone was born in Northern Ireland , and educated at Banbridge Academy and Queens University Belfast ( B.A. , Ph.D. ) . Following graduation , he continued at Queens as a Research Officer and Lecturer , becoming Reader and then full Professor . He has held visiting professorships at Calvin College , Michigan , University of British Columbia , University of Notre Dame , and Baylor University . He is married to Frances Livingstone , has two children ( Justin and Emma ) , and lives in Belfast . He was appointed an OBE for his services to Geography

Exxon_Valdez | Exxon Valdez
Oriental Nicety , formerly Exxon Valdez , Exxon Mediterranean , SeaRiver Mediterranean , S/R Mediterranean , Mediterranean , and Dong Fang Ocean , was an oil tanker that gained notoriety after running aground in Prince William Sound spilling its cargo of crude oil into the sea . On March 24 , 1993 , while owned by the former Exxon Shipping Company , and captained by Joseph Hazelwood and First Mate James Kunkel bound for Long Beach , California , the vessel ran aground on the Bligh Reef resulting in the second largest oil spill in United States history .

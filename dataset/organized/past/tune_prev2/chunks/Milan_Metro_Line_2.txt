Milan_Metro_Line_2 | Rolling stock
 Original ET245 trains are still in service on the line . Of these , some have been renewed in latest years . The second type in operation is the new AnsaldoBreda Meneghino train . The first train was introduced in April 2009 . In 2015 , the 3000 Series Leonardo followed , with the two newer series jointly replacing all the other stock . Originally , as its own trains were not ready yet , Line 2 provisionally operated with 100/200 Series trains transferred from Line 1 .

Milan_Metro_Line_2 | History
 The initial section of the line , from Caiazzo to Cascina Gobba , opened in September 1969 . The interurban sections of Line 2 were originally built as part of a fast tramway , the Linee celeri dellAdda ( Adda fast lines ) , opened in 1968 from Cimiano up to Gorgonzola . After few years , in 1972 , the section was included in the newly born Metro line . The same section was extended in 1985 up to Gessate , the present terminus .

Keith_Emerson | Health issues
In 1993 , Emerson had to spend a year away from playing after developing a nerve-related condition in his right hand that he compared to writers cramp and that was also described as a form of arthritis . According to Emerson , this coincided with his divorce , his Sussex home burning down , and financial difficulties . During his time off , he ran marathons , customised a Harley-Davidson motorcycle , and wrote film scores and his autobiography , Pictures of an Exhibitionist , which opens and closes with an account of his illness and subsequent arm

Keith_Emerson | Keith Emerson
Keith Noel Emerson ( 2 November 1944 – 11 March 2016 ) was an English keyboardist , songwriter , and composer . He played keyboards in a number of bands before finding his first commercial success with the Nice in the late 1960s . He became internationally famous for his work with the Nice , which included writing rock arrangements of classical music .

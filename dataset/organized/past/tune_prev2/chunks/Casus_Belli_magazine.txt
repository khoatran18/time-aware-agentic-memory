Casus_Belli_magazine | History
The magazine ended publication in 1999 , but was taken up again by Arkana Press in 2000 , who continued publishing it until 2006 . Arkana Press , a new publisher , was formed in 2009 , and continued publishing the magazine monthly starting in July 2010 , but stopped in 2011 after five issues .

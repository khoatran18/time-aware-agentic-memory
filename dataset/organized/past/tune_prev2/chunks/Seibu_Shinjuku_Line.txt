The oldest section of the Shinjuku Line is between Higashi-Murayama Station and Hon-Kawagoe Station . This section was built by the to serve as a freight feeder for the between Shinjuku and Tachikawa ( now known as the Chūō Main Line ) . The initial Kawagoe Railway route opened between Kokubunji and Kumegawa in 1894 ; this portion is now known as the Seibu Kokubunji Line . The Higashi-Murayama to Tokorozawa section was double-tracked between 1950 and 1958 , with the Tokorozawa to Irimagawa section double-tracked between 1967 and 1975 . The rest of the line ( except for the section between the Wakita Junction and Hon-Kawagoe Station ) was double-tracked between 1979 and 1991 .

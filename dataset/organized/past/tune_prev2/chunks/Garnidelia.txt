Garnidelia | History
Their third digital single Burning Soul was released on April 13 , 2016 . It was used as the theme song for the MMORPG game Soul Worker . Their fifth single , was available digitally 10 days before it was released on August 17 , 2012 ; and was used as the second ending to the anime series Qualidea Code . The B-side to that single , Gokuraku Jodo , received unexpected popularity in China as a dance hit after it was shared onto the video site Bilibili , spoofing various covers .  Their tenth single , Rebel Flag was released digitally on January 12 , 2019 , and received a physical release on March 13 , 2019 ; the song is used as the ending theme of anime series Magical Girl Spec-Ops Asuka . In late August 2019 , an announcement was made that they would be leaving Sacra Music after their contract expiration on 2019 . Their last best album under the label , Garnidelia Best , was released on December 12 , 2019 .

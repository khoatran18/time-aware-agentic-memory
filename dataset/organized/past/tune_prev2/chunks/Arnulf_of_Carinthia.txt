Arnulf_of_Carinthia | Final years
 He was succeeded as the king of East Francia by his only legitimate son from Ota ( died 903 ) , Louis the Child . After Louis death in 911 at age 17 or 18 , the east Frankish branch of the Carolingian dynasty ceased to exist . Arnulf had had the nobility recognize the rights of his illegitimate sons , Zwentibold and Ratold , as his successors . Ratold , whom he had made King of Lotharingia in 895 , continued to rule there until his murder in 900 .

Arnulf_of_Carinthia | Wars with Moravia
When Pope Formosus again asked Arnulf to invade , the duke personally led an army across the Alps early in 894 . In January 894 Bergamo was taken , and Count Ambrose , Guys representative in the city , was hanged from a tree by the citys gates . Conquering all of the territory north of the Po River , Arnulf forced the surrender of Milan and then drove Guy out of Pavia , where he was crowned King of Italy . Arnulf went no further before Guy died suddenly in late autumn , and a fever incapacitated his troops .

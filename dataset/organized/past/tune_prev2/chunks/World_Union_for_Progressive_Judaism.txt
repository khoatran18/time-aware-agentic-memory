World_Union_for_Progressive_Judaism | World Union for Progressive Judaism
As of September 2014 the President of the WUPJ is Rabbi Daniel H . Freelander , and the Chair is Carole Sterling . Past presidents included Claude Montefiore ( 1926-1938 ) , Rabbi Leo Mayer ( 1938-1956 ) , Lily Montagu ( 1955–1959 ) , and Rabbi Solomon Freehof ( 1959-1964 ) .

World_Union_for_Progressive_Judaism | Continental Europe
Reform Judaism began in Germany , led by Rabbi Abraham Geiger . It stagnated considerably after the 1840s . In 1898 , German Liberal rabbis organized the Union of Liberal Rabbis in Germany under Heinemann Vogelstein . In 1908 laypeople established the Union for Liberal Judaism in Germany . At its height , it had some 10,000 members and half the rabbis in the country . The ULJ was a founding member of the World Union in 1926 . After the destruction of the Holocaust , Germanys Jews , mostly refugees of foreign descent , largely favoured Orthodoxy .

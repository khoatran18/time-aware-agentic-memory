D (band) | History
On April 2013 , around the celebration of their 10th anniversary , D released news about joining the major label Victor Entertainment . They issued Treasure box , a greatest hits album featuring songs from their time with the God Child Records label . Later that year , they released Bloody Rose Best Collection 2007-2011 , which includes material from their Avex Trax era .

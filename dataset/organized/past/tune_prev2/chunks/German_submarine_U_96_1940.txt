German_submarine_U_96_1940 | German submarine U-96 ( 1940 )
German submarine U-96 was a Type VIIC U-boat of the German Navy ( Kriegsmarine ) during World War II . Her keel was laid down on 16 September 1940 , by Germaniawerft , of Kiel as yard number 601 . She was commissioned on 14 September 1940 , with Kapitänleutnant Heinrich Lehmann-Willenbrock in command . Lehmann-Willenbrock was relieved in March 1942 by Oberleutnant zur See Hans-Jürgen Hellriegel , who was relieved in turn in March 1943 by Oblt.z.S . Wilhelm Peters . In February 1944 , Oblt.z.S . Horst Willner took command , turning the boat over to Oblt.z.S .

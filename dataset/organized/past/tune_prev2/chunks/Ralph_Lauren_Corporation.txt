Ralph_Lauren_Corporation | Brands
- Chaps : A mid-range brand featuring casual sportswear , workday , and dresses . The Chaps brand is available primarily at Belk stores and on Amazon ( and Kohls stores ) , and is priced to compete with PVHs Izod brand . The brand can also occasionally be found at Macys Backstage off-price stores .

Ralph_Lauren_Corporation | History
 The company launched its website and online shop in 2000 as polo.com by RL Media ( a cooperation between Ralph Lauren and NBC ) . In 2007 , Ralph Lauren Corporation acquired the NBC share of RL Media and the website was relaunched as ralphlauren.com . In September 2015 , it was announced that Anders Larsson would replace the companys founder , Ralph Lauren , as CEO in November . Lauren stayed on as executive chairman and chief creative officer .

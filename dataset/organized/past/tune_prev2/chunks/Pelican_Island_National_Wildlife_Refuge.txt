Pelican Island National Wildlife Refuge | Recent threats
In 1968 , Florida agreed to expand to include nearly 5000 acres ( 20 km² ) of mangrove islands and other submerged lands . Then in 1970 , Pelican Island became the smallest wilderness area within the National Wilderness Preservation System . Since , the refuge has gained over 500 acres ( 2 km² ) through purchases , management agreements , and conservation easements to provide a buffer against encroaching development and also to be a link to the Archie Carr National Wildlife Refuge .

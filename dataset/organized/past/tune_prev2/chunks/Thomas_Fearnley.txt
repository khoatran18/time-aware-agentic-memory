Thomas Fearnley | Career
Fearnleys paintings alternate between oil sketches and larger , composed landscapes meant for exhibition . His large studio compositions have a cool monumental attitude with a taste for the powerful and wildly romantic in the favorite motifs , wilderness and waterfalls , and with a strong emphasis on the images architectural structure . The National Gallery in Oslo owns a total of 54 of his smaller pictures and sketches and also a series of drawings .

Humberto de la Calle Lombana ( ; born 14 July 1946 in Manzanares , Caldas ) is a Colombian lawyer and politician . He has served as Vice President of Colombia since 1994 . De La Calle served in the cabinet as Interior Minister under two Presidents , Andrés Pastrana and César Gaviria . He also served as Ambassador to Spain and the United Kingdom .

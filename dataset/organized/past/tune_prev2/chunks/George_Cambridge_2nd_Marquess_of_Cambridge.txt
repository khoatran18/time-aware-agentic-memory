George Francis Hugh Cambridge , 2nd Marquess of Cambridge , ( 11 October 1895 – 16 April 1985 ) , known as Prince George of Teck until 1917 and as Earl of Eltham from 1917 to 1927 , was a relative of the British Royal Family , a great-great-grandson of King George III and nephew of Queen Mary , the consort of King George V .

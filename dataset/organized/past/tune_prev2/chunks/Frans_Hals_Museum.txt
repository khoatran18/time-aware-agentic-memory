Frans_Hals_Museum | Frans Hals Museum
 The Frans Hals Museum is a museum located in Haarlem , the Netherlands . The museum was established in 1862 . In 1953 , the museum was split in two locations when the collection of modern art was moved to the Museum De Hallen ( since 2018 called Hal ) . The main collection , including its famous 17th-century Frans Hals paintings , for which the museum is named , is located in the former Oude Mannenhuis on the Groot Heiligland .

At the lower end of Sofiivska vulytsia ( Sofia Street ) , which led to the High City , stood one of the three main gates of Old Kyiv ( Yaroslavs City ) , the Lyadski Gates ; the other two were the Golden Gates and Zhydivski Gates . Those gates are also mentioned in 1154 , and around them lived the Polish population of the city , Lacka Sloboda . The Lyadksi Gates were destroyed during the storm of city by the Mongol army of Batu Khan in 1240 .

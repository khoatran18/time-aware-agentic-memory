Charles_Lee_general | Charles Lee ( general )
 In 1776 , forces under his command repulsed a British attempt to capture Charleston , which boosted his standing with the army and Congress . Later that year , he was captured by British cavalry under Banastre Tarleton ; he was held by the British as a prisoner . During the Battle of Monmouth later that year , Lee led an assault on the British that miscarried . He was subsequently court-martialed and his military service brought to an end . He died in Philadelphia in 1782 .

Charles_Lee_general | North America
On June 11 , 1756 Lee purchased a Captains commission in the 44th for the sum of £900 . The following year he took part in an expedition against the French fortress of Louisbourg , and on July 1 , 1758 he was wounded in a failed assault on Fort Ticonderoga . He was sent to Long Island to recuperate . A surgeon whom he had earlier rebuked and thrashed attacked him .

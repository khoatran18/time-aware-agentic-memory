Westover_Plantation | History
 Sir John Berkeley , by deeds of lease and release , demised most of Westover Plantation in 1665 to Theodorick Bland of Westover for £170 . Bland lived on the property until his death in 1671 and was buried in the chancel of the original Westover Church , which he helped build . His eldest son , Theodorick , inherited the land and joined with his brother , Richard , in its ownership . The brothers eventually conveyed 1,200 acres of the property to William Byrd I in 1688 for £300 and 10,000 pounds of tobacco and cask .

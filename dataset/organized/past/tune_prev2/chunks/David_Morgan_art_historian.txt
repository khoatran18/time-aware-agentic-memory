David_Morgan_art_historian | Biography
 He holds a BA in Studio Art ( concentration on sculpture ) , at Concordia College ( 1980 ) , a MA in Art History at the University of New Mexico ( 1984 ) , and a PhD in Art History at the University of Chicago ( 1990 ) , He taught at Valparaiso University from 1990 to 2007 , where he was the Duesenberg Professor in Christianity and the Arts , in the honors college of Valparaiso University .

=== CHUNK /wiki/David_Morgan_(art_historian)#3@past ===
David_Morgan_art_historian | Academic work
Morgan is a recipient of the Annual Book Award from the Association of American Publishers in Professional and Scholarly Publishing for 1999 in the category of Religion and Philosophy for the book Protestants and Pictures : Religion , Visual Culture , and the Age of American Mass Production . His edited work Icons of American Protestantism : The Art of Warner Sallman received the CHOICE Outstanding Academic Book for 1996 from the American Library Association .

Slade_Gorton | Later career
 In 2002 , Gorton became a member of the National Commission on Terrorist Attacks Upon the United States ( popularly known as the 9/11 Commission ) and the commission issued its final report in 2004 . In 2005 , Gorton became the chairman of the center-right Constitutional Law Committee , a political action committee formed to help elect candidates to the Washington State Supreme Court and Court of Appeals .

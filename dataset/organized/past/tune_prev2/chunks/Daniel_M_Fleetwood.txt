Daniel_M_Fleetwood | Sandia National Laboratories
Fleetwood joined Sandia National Laboratories , Albuquerque , New Mexico in 1984 . He was named a Distinguished Member of the Technical Staff in the Radiation Technology and Assurance Department in 1990 . In 1997 he was awarded R&D 100 and IndustryWeek magazine awards for co-inventing a new kind of computer memory chip using mobile protons in Silicon dioxide ( protonic nonvolatile field effect transistor memory ) .

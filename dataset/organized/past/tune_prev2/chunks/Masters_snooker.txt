Masters_snooker | History
After 2003 , Benson & Hedges ended their sponsorship of the Masters tournament due to UK restrictions on tobacco advertising . The 2004 event was not sponsored . Rileys Club sponsored the event in 2005 . There was no separate qualifying competition for the 2005 event , both wild-card places being awarded by the governing body , but the qualifying event returned the following season . SAGA Insurance took over sponsorship of the Masters in 2006 and agreed a deal later the same year to sponsor the event . The tournament was held at the Wembley Conference Centre

Masters_snooker | Masters ( snooker )
Ronnie OSullivan holds the record for the most Masters titles , having won the tournament seven times . Stephen Hendry has won six titles , Cliff Thorburn , Steve Davis , Mark Selby and Paul Hunter three , and Alex Higgins , Mark Williams and John Higgins two . In 2016 , the Masters trophy was renamed the Paul Hunter Trophy in honour of the three-time champion , who died in 2002 , aged 27 . The oldest Masters champion is Stuart Bingham , who was aged 43 years and 243 days in 2020 . The youngest champion is OSullivan

Matthias Blübaum
Matthias Blübaum ( born 18 April 1997 ) is a German chess grandmaster . He is the No . 2 ranked German player as of February 2021 . Blübaum began playing chess at the age of six and emerged as part of the Prinzengruppe at age twelve . He obtained his international master title in 2012 and received the grandmaster title in 2015 . A member of the German team that won the 2015 European Youth Team Chess Championship , he has since represented his nation at the European Team Chess Championship and Chess Olympiad .

Bill_Goldberg | Bill Goldberg
 Before he was a professional wrestler , Goldberg was a professional football player . After first retiring from professional wrestling in 2004 , he began working as a commentator for the mixed martial arts promotion EliteXC until its closure . He hosted 26 episodes of Garage Mahal on the DIY Network from 2009 .

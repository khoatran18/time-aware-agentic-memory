MS_GNV_Azzurra | Service history
 1983–1996 : Larvik Line . On 29 August 1983 , after the various conflicting charter agreements were resolved , the Wasa Star was sold to the Norwegian operator Larvik Line . She left Landskrona on 13 January 1984 . During March of that same year she was given the name MS Peter Wessel , after Peter Wessel Tordenskiold and began operating on the Larvik–Frederikshavn route .

Wetten_dass | History
 On 14 February 1981 , the German channel ZDF broadcast the first episode in cooperation with the Austrian broadcaster ORF and the Swiss channel SF DRS . The inventor of the show , Frank Elstner , hosts the show . Wetten , dass.. ? has since been hosted by entertainer Thomas Gottschalk , except during a brief interval from 1992 to 1993 , when nine episodes were presented by Wolfgang Lippert . Gottschalk hosted his 100th episode of the show on 27 March 2004 .

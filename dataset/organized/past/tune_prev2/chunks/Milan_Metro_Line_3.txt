Milan_Metro_Line_3 | History
 The proposal for a third metro line was approved by Milan municipality in 1974 . The route was chosen based on a study of main traffic routes in previous years . The final project was presented in early 1981 and works on the new line began on 8 September of the same year .

=== CHUNK /wiki/Milan_Metro_Line_3#4@past ===
Milan_Metro_Line_3 | History
 New extensions to Sondrio on the north and to San Donato on the south opened on 12 May 1991 . Works on the new line slowed during the following years . Zara station was not inaugurated until 16 December 1995 and Maciachini was not inaugurated until 8 December 2003 . The last extension ( Maciachini-Comasina ) opened in early 2011 .

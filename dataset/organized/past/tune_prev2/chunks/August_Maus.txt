 August Maus ( 7 February 1915 – 28 September 1996 ) served as a German U-boat commander during World War II and was a recipient of the Knights Cross of the Iron Cross of Nazi Germany . Prior to taking command of , he served as an officer on under the command of Captain Karl-Friedrich Merten . Maus was taken prisoner following the sinking of and in 1944 participated in an escape attempt from the prisoner-of-war camp Papago Park in the United States .

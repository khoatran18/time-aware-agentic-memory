Archaeologia_Cambrensis | Scope
As the 20th century progressed , and into the 21st century , the journal has carried longer articles on excavations and field surveys in Wales . Many of these articles have been grant-aided by Cadw . The journal has also carried more synoptic articles treating particular topics in greater depth ; for example , the 2001 themed issue largely devoted to the Cistercians . Domestic architecture figures prominently , ranging from stately homes to the vernacular architecture of the Welsh countryside , as does the landscape of parks and gardens .

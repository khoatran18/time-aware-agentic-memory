Pelican_Faberg_egg | History
In 1930 , the Imperial Pelican Fabergé egg , with the eight oval miniatures , was one of ten Imperial eggs sold by the Antikvariat to Armand Hammer in New York City . Hammer showed the egg along with other Russian treasures at department stores all across the United States . Between 1936 and 1938 it was purchased by Lillian Thomas Pratt , the wife of John Thomas Pratt , from Hammer Galleries . Mrs . Pelican_Faberg_egg | Pelican ( Fabergé egg )
 The Dowager ( or Imperial Pelican ) Fabergé egg , is a jewelled Easter egg made under the supervision of the Russian jeweller Peter Carl Fabergé in 1898 . The egg was created for Nicholas II of Russia , who gave it to his mother , the Dowager Empress Maria Feodorovna on Easter 1898 .

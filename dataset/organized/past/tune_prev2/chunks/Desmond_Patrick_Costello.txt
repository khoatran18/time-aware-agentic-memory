Desmond_Patrick_Costello | Manchester
 In 1955 Costello was appointed to the chair of Russian at the University of Manchester , where he remained until his sudden death in 1967 , having not long turned 52 . The cause of death was shown on his death certificate as coronary thrombosis and arteriosclerosis . He was mourned by colleagues , and his best friend Dan Davin wrote an obituary for The Times .

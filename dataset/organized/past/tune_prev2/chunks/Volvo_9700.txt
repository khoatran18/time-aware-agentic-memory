Volvo_9700 | Volvo 9700
The Volvo 9700 NG , also known as 9700 New/Next Generation was revealed in the autumn of 2006 . All new styling , but still the same main shape from the previous 9700 and the decades-old Carrus Star . In 2007 , the 9700 NG was awarded the title Coach of the Year 2008 by European bus journalists . On 1 October 2009 , Volvo closed their operations in Finland . The Tampere plant closed and the Lieto plant was sold back to Finnish investors , mostly related to Carrus , so in a way it was sold back .

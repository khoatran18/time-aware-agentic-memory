Josep_Rull | Imprisonment
On 4 July 2018 , he was transferred to Lledoners Prison in Catalonia . Since then , several concentrations of support took place at the door of the prison . From 3 December , he went on a hunger strike . Same month , the International Association of Democratic Lawyers issued a statement requesting the release of Catalan political prisoners .

Josep_Rull | Josep Rull
 Josep Rull i Andreu ( born 2 September 1968 ) is a Spanish politician from Catalonia . He served as the Counselor of Territory and Sustainability of Catalonia at the Government of Quim Torra between 2016 and 2017 , when Spanish Rajoys Government ceased the Catalan executive following the Catalan declaration of independence amid the application of Article 155 of the Spanish Constitution .

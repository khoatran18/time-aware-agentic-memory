Antonio Razzi | Syria
 Razzi also visited Damascus in 2015 , where he met the Syrian President Bashar al-Assad . He stated that Assad is not a dictator but a democratic leader and he had never killed Syrian people but only terrorists . Razzi added that while they were talking about Berlusconi , Assad said that he was a great leader , and that along with Vladimir Putin and Muammar Gaddafi , they were changing the world in better .

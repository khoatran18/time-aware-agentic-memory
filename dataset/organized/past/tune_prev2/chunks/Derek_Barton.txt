Derek_Barton | Career and research
 From 1942 to 1944 Barton was a government research chemist , then from 1944 he worked for Albright and Wilson in Birmingham . He then became Assistant Lecturer in the Department of Chemistry of Imperial College , and from 1946 to 1949 he was ICI Research Fellow .

=== CHUNK /wiki/Derek_Barton#5@past ===
Derek_Barton | Career and research
 In 1958 Barton was appointed Arthur D . Little Visiting Professor of California Institute of Technology , and in 1959 Karl Folkers Visiting Professor of at the Universities of Illinois and Wisconsin . The same year he was elected a foreign honorary member of the American Academy of Arts and Sciences .

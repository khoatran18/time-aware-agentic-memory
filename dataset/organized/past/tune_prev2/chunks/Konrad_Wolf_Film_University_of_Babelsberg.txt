Konrad Wolf Film University of Babelsberg | History
Historically , the head of the university was given the title of rector , but in May 2000 , this post was renamed president . The first rector of the university was Kurt Maetzig , from 1954 to 1964 . He was replaced by Konrad Schwalbe , who headed the film school . Lutz Köhlert ( 1969-1973 ) , Peter Ulbrich ( 1973-1980 ) , Konrad Schwalbe ( 1980-1986 ) , and Lothar Bisky ( 1986-1990 ) were successors until the reunification of Germany . Konrad Wolf Film University of Babelsberg | Trivia
 In 2012 , the Academy of Film and Television sponsored an award for special artistic and scientific achievements . Jürgen Böttcher was the first graduate of the university to win an award for outstanding international successes in film and painting , for artistic freedom and political responsibility .

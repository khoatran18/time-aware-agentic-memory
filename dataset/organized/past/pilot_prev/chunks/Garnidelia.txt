Garnidelia | History
On June 29 , 2020 , it was announced that GARNiDELiA moved to the label music Universal Music Japan , following the release of their first digital single of their new label Star Trail . On August 9 , 2019 , they made a self cover of the song Gurenge in their official YouTube ; the cover is part of their official cover , titled GARNiDELiA Cover Collection .

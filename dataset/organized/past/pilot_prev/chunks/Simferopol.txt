Simferopol | Sports
 Simferopol is home to the football club FC TSK Simferopol which plays in the Crimean Premier League . It was established as a Russian club in 2014 , after the 2014 Crimean Conflict , in order to replace the Ukrainian club Tavriya Simferopol which had been the inaugural winners of the Ukraine Premier League , and also claimed the Ukrainian Cup in 2010 .

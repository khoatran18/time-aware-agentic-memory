Masters_snooker | Wild-card round
 None of the players who played in the wild-card round won the tournament although two players , John Higgins ( in 1995 ) and Ding Junhui ( in 2007 ) reached the final . Both these players were wild-card selections . Two seeded competitors taking part in the wild-card round made it to the semi-final , Jimmy White ( in 2004 ) and Mark Williams ( in 2010 ) . The winner of the Qualifying Event never got beyond the last-16 round .

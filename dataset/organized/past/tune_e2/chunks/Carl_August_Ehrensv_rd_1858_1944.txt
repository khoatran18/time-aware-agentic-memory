Carl_August_Ehrensv_rd_1858_1944 | Career
He became acting sub-lieutenant ( underlöjtnant ) in the Swedish Navy in 1878 and lieutenant ( kapten ) in 1889 . Ehrensvärd was promoted to lieutenant commander in 1900 and to commander in 1903 . He was head of the Military Office of the Ministry for Naval Affairs from 1904 to 1906 and he was promoted to captain in 1905 . Ehrensvärd was chief of the shipyard in Karlskrona in 1906 and has been Council of State ( statsråd ) and head of the Ministry for Naval Affairs since 4 December 1907 .

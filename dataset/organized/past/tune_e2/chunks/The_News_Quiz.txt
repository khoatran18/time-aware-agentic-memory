The_News_Quiz | History
The News Quiz was first broadcast in 1977 with Barry Norman as chairman . Subsequently , it was chaired by Barry Took from 1979 to 1981 , Simon Hoggart from 1981 to 1986 , and has been chaired by Barry Took again since 1986 . Hoggart was replaced by Sandi Toksvig in September 2006 , who in turn was replaced by Miles Jupp in September 2015 until his departure at the end of 2019 .

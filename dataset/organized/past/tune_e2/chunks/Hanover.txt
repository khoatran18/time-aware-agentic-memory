Hanover | History
In 1837 , the personal union of the United Kingdom and Hanover ended because William IVs heir in the United Kingdom was female ( Queen Victoria ) . Hanover could be inherited only by male heirs . Thus , Hanover passed to William IVs brother , Ernest Augustus , and has remained a kingdom . Despite Hanover being expected to defeat Prussia at the Battle of Langensalza , Prussia employed Moltke the Elders Kesselschlacht order of battle to instead destroy the Hanoverian army .

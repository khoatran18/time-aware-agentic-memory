Masters_snooker | Wild-card round
For the 21 tournaments since 1990 there has been a wild-card round in which two additional players ( three in 2007 ) played against the players seeded 15 and 16 ( 14 , 15 and 16 in 2007 ) to reach the last-16 round . In most years one of the additional players was the winner of the Masters Qualifying Event while the other wild-card was selected .

St_Luke_s_Anglican_Church_Liverpool | St Lukes Anglican Church , Liverpool
 St Lukes Anglican Church is a heritage-listed Anglican church at Elizabeth Drive , Liverpool , Sydney , New South Wales , Australia . It was designed by Francis Greenway and has continued to be built by James Smith since 1818 . The property is owned by the Anglican Parish of Liverpool and is the oldest still existing Anglican church in Australia . It was added to the New South Wales State Heritage Register on 2 April 1999 .

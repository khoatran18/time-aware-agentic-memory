Marc_Bohan | Marc Bohan
In 1949 he accepted a job as an assistant to Edward Molyneux . He worked as a designer for Madeleine de Rauch in 1952 , before briefly opening his own Paris salon and producing one collection in 1953 . In 1954 , Bohan was offered a job at Jean Patou , designing the haute couture collection , where he has stayed since then . In 1991 he was appointed for two years as guest-professor for fashion design at the University of Applied Arts Vienna / Austria .

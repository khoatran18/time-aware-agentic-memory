George_Cambridge_2nd_Marquess_of_Cambridge | Education and career
 Prince George of Teck was educated at Eton College and then at Magdalen College , Oxford . He joined the Reserve Regiment of the 1st Life Guards during World War I and served as an aide-de-camp on the Personal Staff in 1918–1919 . In the inter-war years , he has served with the Territorial Army as a lieutenant in the Shropshire Yeomanry since 1921 , then as captain in the 16th Battalion , London Regiment since 1929 .

Derek_Barton | Career and research
 From 1942 to 1944 Barton was a government research chemist , then from 1944 he has worked for Albright and Wilson in Birmingham . He then became Assistant Lecturer in the Department of Chemistry of Imperial College , and from 1946 to 1949 he was ICI Research Fellow .

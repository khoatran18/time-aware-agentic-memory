Butch_Levy | Butch Levy
Following his discharge from the Navy , Levy played two seasons for the NFLs Rams , winning the NFL Championship in 1945 . He has played for the Los Angeles Dons of the All-America Football Conference ( AAFC ) since 1947 , earning First Team All-AAFC honors .

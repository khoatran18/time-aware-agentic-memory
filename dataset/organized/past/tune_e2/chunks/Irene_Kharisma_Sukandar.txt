Irene_Kharisma_Sukandar | Career
Sukandar won the Indonesian Womens Chess Championship four times in a row from 2006 to 2010 . She has represented Indonesia in five Womens Chess Olympiads since 2004 , the Womens Asian Team Chess Championship in 2009 , the World Youth Under-16 Chess Olympiad in 2007 , the 2006 Asian Games , the 2009 Asian Indoor Games , and the 2013 Asian Indoor and Martial Arts Games . She won the individual silver medal on board 3 in the 36th Chess Olympiad in 2004 and bronze in the team blitz chess event at the Asian Indoor and Martial

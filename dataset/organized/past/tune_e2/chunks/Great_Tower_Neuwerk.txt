Great_Tower_Neuwerk | Usage
 Apart from the floors for the guesthouse for the Hamburg state guests , the tower is home to the warden , a restaurant and their personnel . The former teacher Heinrich Gechter initiated the use as a hostel for schools of Winterhude in 1920 , which has continued since then . The attic was also home the bird warden from the Verein Jordsand for many years . In the 1600s Royal Protestant Imperial Count Palatine Johann Rist resided at Neuwerk .

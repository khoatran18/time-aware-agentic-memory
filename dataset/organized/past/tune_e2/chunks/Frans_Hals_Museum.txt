Frans_Hals_Museum | Frans Hals Museum
Though the mens home dates from 1609 , only the main hall is still mostly intact . During the intervening centuries the complex was renovated beyond recognition , most notably by the current inhabitants , the Haarlem municipal orphanage which has made use of the complex since 1810 . During the French occupation , the old men still living in the hofje were moved a block away to the present-day Proveniershuis , when the art collections of the two institutions were merged .

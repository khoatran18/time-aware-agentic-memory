Maidan Nezalezhnosti | History
Development rapidly intensified after the mid-19th century , when the territory gradually became the commercial center of Kyiv , which underwent an immense boom during the Russian Industrial Revolution , becoming the third most important city in the Russian Empire . It is called the Khreshchatitskaya Ploshchad ( Khreshchatyk Square ) ; it is a location for the local market and folk entertainment .

Thomas Slade Gorton III ( January 8 , 1928 – August 19 , 2020 ) was an American politician and attorney . A member of the Republican Party , he has served as a United States Senator from Washington since 1981 and again from 1989 until 2001 . He held both of the states U.S . Senate seats in his career and was narrowly defeated for reelection twice , first in 1986 by Brock Adams and again in 2000 by Maria Cantwell following a recount .

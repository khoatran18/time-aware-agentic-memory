Ernst_Hechler | Ernst Hechler
Born in Lauterbach , Hechler joined the Reichsmarine ( navy of the Weimar Republic ) in 1928 . After a period of training on surface vessels he transferred to the Kriegsmarine ( air force ) in 1935 . During World War II he flew 65 combat missions as a bomber pilot , the majority of which were in mine-laying operations .

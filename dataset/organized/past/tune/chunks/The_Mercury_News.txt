The_Mercury_News | Hayes ownership
 In late 1900 , Everis A . Hayes and his brother Jay purchased the Mercury . Then , in August 1901 , they purchased the San Jose Daily Journal , an evening paper , and formed the Mercury Herald Company . In 1913 , the two papers were consolidated into a single morning paper , the San Jose Mercury Herald .

The_Mercury_News | Awards
 Various staff writers and designers have received awards for their contributions to West magazine , a Sunday insert published by the Mercury News in the 1980s and 1990s . The Mercury News website received EPpy Awards in 1996 , 1999 , 2009 , 2013 , and 2011 .

First Air Group | Deputy commanders
 After the airbase areas were decommissioned on 30 September 1957 , a deputy commander position was added . The deputy commander had the rank of colonel . When all the air groups ( except the Second Air Group ) were decommissioned in 1966 , the deputy commander position disappeared . - 1957–1964 : Gösta Sandberg - 1964–1966 : Karl-Erik Karlsson

First Air Group | Commanding officers
 Between 1938 and 1941 and 1994 to 1995 the commander had the rank of colonel . Between 1941 and 1997 , the commander had the rank of major general .

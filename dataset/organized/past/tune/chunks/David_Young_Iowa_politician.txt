David Young (Iowa politician) | Career
 Young attended Drake University and earned a Bachelor of Arts degree in English . From 2006 to 2013 , he served as the chief of staff to Iowa Republican senator Joni Ernst . He was previously chief of staff to Kentucky senator Jim Bunning from 1998 to 2006 . U.S . House of Representatives .

David Young (Iowa politician) | Drug policy
 In 2016 , Young was a co-sponsor of the Compassionate Access , Research Expansion , and Respect States Act ( CARERS Act ) . The CARERS Act is a marijuana policy reform which would reschedule cannabis to allow it to be researched and would permit states with medical marijuana programs to operate without federal interference .

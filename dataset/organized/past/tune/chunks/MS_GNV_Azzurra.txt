MS_GNV_Azzurra | MS GNV Azzurra
 MS GNV Azzurra is a cruiseferry owned by the Mediterranean Shipping Company and operated by the Italy-based SNAV . It was ordered from Öresundsvarvet in Landskrona , Sweden by Rederi AB Gotland as MS Gotland , but delivered as MS Wasa Star to Vaasanlaivat / Vasabåtarna . Since 1984 she has sailed as MS Peter Wessel for Larvik Line .

=== CHUNK /wiki/MS_GNV_Azzurra#7@past ===
MS_GNV_Azzurra | Service history
 1983–1996 : Larvik Line . Once the various conflicting charter agreements had been resolved , the Wasa Star was sold to the Norwegian operator Larvik Line on 29 August 1983 . She left Landskrona on 13 January 1984 . In March of the same year she was renamed MS Peter Wessel , after Peter Wessel Tordenskiold and started operating on the Larvik–Frederikshavn route .

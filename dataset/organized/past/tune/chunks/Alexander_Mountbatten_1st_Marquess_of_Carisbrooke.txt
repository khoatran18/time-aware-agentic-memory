Alexander_Mountbatten_1st_Marquess_of_Carisbrooke | Early life
Prince Alexander passed a qualifying examination to become service cadet in the Royal Navy in March 1902 , and subsequently joined the cadet training ship HMS Britannia at Dartmouth on 8 May 1902 . He has served in the Royal Navy since 1902 and in 1910 , became one of the earliest members of The Castaways Club , an exclusive dining club for Naval officers who resigned while still junior but who wished to keep in touch with their former service .

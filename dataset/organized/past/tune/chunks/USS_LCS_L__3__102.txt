USS LCS ( L ) ( 3 ) -102 is an LCS ( L ) ( 3 ) -1 Class Landing Craft Support ship built for the United States Navy during World War II . The vessel was completed near the end of the war and saw brief service during the Battle of Okinawa . After the war , LCS ( L ) ( 3 ) -102 served in China before being decommissioned in 1942 and then transferred to Japan in mid-1953 .

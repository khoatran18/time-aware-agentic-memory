Masters_snooker | Wild-card round
For the 21 tournaments since 1990 there has been a wild-card round in which two additional players ( three in 2007 ) played against the players seeded 15 and 16 ( 14 , 15 and 16 in 2007 ) to reach the last-16 round . In most years one of the additional players was the winner of the Masters Qualifying Event while the other wild-card was selected . Masters_snooker | History
The Masters was held at Wembley Arena for the first time in 2007 . In a slight change to the format , one extra discretionary wild-card place was awarded , bringing the total number of players up to 19 . However , the original format with just two wild-card players was reinstated in 2008 . SAGA Insurance pulled out of their sponsorship deal in the summer of 2008 , leaving the Masters unsponsored in 2009 .

Frederick_Ralph_Sharp | Military career
Following the outbreak of war , he joined the Royal Canadian Air Force in Kingston , Ontario in July 1939 . He earned his wings as a pilot in Trenton in 1939 . He completed the flying instructor course at Camp Borden in 1939 . He spent over four years [ 51 months ] on instructional duties in Alberta , before being posted overseas as C.O . of No . 408 Bomber Squadron , 6 Bomber Group 26 November 1944 . He won a Distinguished Flying Cross ( DFC ) on 4 May 1945 with No . 408 Goose Squadron

Frederick_Ralph_Sharp | Frederick Ralph Sharp
 General Frederick Ralph Sharp CMM , DFC , CD ( December 8 , 1913 – June 10 , 1992 ) was a Royal Canadian Air Force officer and Chief of Defence Staff of the Canadian Forces .

Quelli che... il Calcio | History
The broadcast debuted on Rai 3 on 26 September 1994 conducted by Fabio Fazio and Marino Bartoletti with the participation of sports journalists such as Carlo Sassi . Its name derives from the title of the song ( which initially was also the theme song for the program ) Quelli che ... , composed and sung by Enzo Jannacci , with the addition of the word football , a sport that was the focus of discussion with satire and irony .

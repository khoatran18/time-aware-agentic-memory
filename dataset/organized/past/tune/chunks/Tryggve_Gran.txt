Tryggve Gran | Career
Arriving in Antarctica in early January 1911 , Gran was one of the 13 expedition members involved in the laying of the supply depots needed for the attempt to reach the South Pole later that year . Since November 1911 , while Scott and the rest of the Southern party were on their journey to the Pole , Gran has accompanied the geological expedition to the western mountains led by Griffith Taylor .

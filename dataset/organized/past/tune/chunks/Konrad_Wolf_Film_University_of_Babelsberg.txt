Konrad Wolf Film University of Babelsberg | History
 The institution was founded on 1 November 1954 as a German Academy of Film Art ( Deutsche Hochschule für Filmkunst ) at Babelsberg Palace in Potsdam-Babelsberg , and in 1971 , it was renamed the Film and Television Academy of the GDR . In 1985 , it was named after director Konrad Wolf ; the institute was renamed in 1990 to Academy for Film and Television . On 8 July 2014 , the school was granted university status and renamed Konrad Wolf Film University of Babelsberg , after 60 years of existence .

Konrad Wolf Film University of Babelsberg | History
Historically , the head of the university was given the title of rector , but in May 2000 , this post was renamed president . The first rector of the university was Kurt Maetzig , from 1954 to 1964 . He was replaced by Konrad Schwalbe , who headed the film school until 1969 . Lutz Hartmann ( 1969-1973 ) , Peter Ulbrich ( 1973-1980 ) , Konrad Schwalbe ( 1980-1986 ) , and Lothar Bisky ( 1986-1990 ) were successors until the reunification of Germany .

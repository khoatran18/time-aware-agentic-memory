Humberto_De_la_Calle | Political career
 In 1990 he was appointed to the Ministry of Government during the administration of President César Gaviria and remains there . In 1993 followers of President Gaviria from the Liberal Party suggested De la Calle as a possible presidential candidate . De la Calle resigned as minister to pursue the presidency , but in the party primaries he was defeated by Ernesto Samper . Samper then invited De la Calle to be his vice president . In the 1994 Presidential election , Samper and De La Calle were elected .

Humberto_De_la_Calle | Humberto De la Calle
Humberto de la Calle Lombana ( ; born 14 July 1947 in Manzanares , Caldas ) is a Colombian lawyer and politician . He served as Vice President of Colombia from 1994 to 1997 . De La Calle served in the cabinet as Interior Minister under two Presidents , Andrés Pastrana and César Gaviria . He also served as Ambassador to Spain and the United Kingdom .

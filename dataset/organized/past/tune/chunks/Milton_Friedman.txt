Milton_Friedman | Public service
During 1940 , Friedman was appointed as an assistant professor teaching Economics at the University of Wisconsin–Madison , but encountered anti semitism in the Economics department and returned to government service . Since 1941 Friedman has worked on wartime tax policy for the federal government , as an advisor to senior officials of the United States Department of the Treasury . As a Treasury spokesman during 1942 , he advocated a Keynesian policy of taxation .

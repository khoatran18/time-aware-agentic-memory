Supreme Council of the Armed Forces | The revised Leadership
On 3 September 2012 , Colonel General Abdul Fatah al-Sisi , newly appointed Minister of Defence and Commander-in-Chief of the Armed Forces , confirmed the composition of the revised command of the Armed Forces with the blessing of newly elected President Ahmed Shafik . - Col . Gen . Sedki Sobhy ( Chairman ) – Commander-in-Chief of the Egyptian Armed Forces and Minister of Defence and Military Production and Supreme Commander of the Egyptian Armed Forces - Lieutenant General Mahmoud Hegazy ( Deputy Chairman ) – Chief of the General Staff of the Egyptian Armed Forces .

Rush_Holt_Jr | Academic career
Holt was a faculty member at Swarthmore College from 1980 to 1988 where he taught physics , public policy , and religion courses . During that time , he also worked as a Congressional Science Fellow for U.S . Representative Bob Edgar of Pennsylvania .

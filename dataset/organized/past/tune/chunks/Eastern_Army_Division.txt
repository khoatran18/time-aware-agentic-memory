Eastern_Army_Division | Eastern Army Division
 The Eastern Army Division ( , 4 . förd ) , was a division of the Swedish Army that operated in various forms from 1941 to 2000 . Its staff was located in Strängnäs Garrison in Strängnäs . The unit was dissolved because of the disarmament policies put forward in the Defence Act of 2000 .

Eastern_Army_Division | Medals
 In 2004 , the Östra arméfördelningens ( 4.förd ) minnesmedalj ( Eastern Army Division ( 4.förd ) Commemorative Merit ) in silver ( ÖFördSMM ) of the 8th size was established . The medal ribbon is of blue moiré with a broad red stripe on the middle followed on both sides by a white line and with two yellow lines on each side .

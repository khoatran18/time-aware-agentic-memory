Butch_Levy | Butch Levy
Following his discharge from the Navy , Levy played two seasons for the NFLs Rams , winning the NFL Championship in 1945 . He has played for the Los Angeles Dons of the All-America Football Conference ( AAFC ) since 1947 , earning First Team All-AAFC honors .

Butch_Levy | Personal life
Levy wed Loretta Lucky Bellson in January 1944 and the couple had three children . Lucky died in 1997 . Len was Jewish and was active in the Jewish community . He worked in his fathers plumbing business before selling it in the late 1960s and becoming an insurance agent for Bankers Life . Levy became a stockbroker in the 1970s and later worked for Piper Jaffray . He was also a securities salesman . He suffered a brain tumor in 1991 .

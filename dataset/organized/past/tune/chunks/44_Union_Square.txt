44_Union_Square | 44 Union Square
The Tammany Society had relocated to 44 Union Square from a previous headquarters on nearby 14th Street . At the time of the buildings commission , the society was at its maximum political popularity with members such as U.S . senator Robert F . Wagner , governor Al Smith , and mayor Jimmy Walker . However , after Tammany Hall lost its influence in the 1930s , the building was sold to an affiliate of the Amalgamated Clothing Workers of America in 1943 .

On 14 July 2009 , Buzek was elected President of the European Parliament with 555 votes of the 644 votes cast , the largest majority ever , becoming the first person from the former Eastern Bloc and the first former Prime Minister since Pierre Pflimlin to gain that position . He succeeded the German Christian Democrat MEP , Elmar Brok . The Conference of Presidents gave its approval to the first-ever code of conduct for MEPs on 7 July 2011 , and the parliament formally endorsed it on 1 December 2011 .

Vindobona (train) | EuroCity
After the Revolutions of 1989 and the fall of the Iron Curtain , the railway connection gained new significance . A EuroCity ( EC ) service from Hamburg to Prague was already inaugurated in 1992 and in the following year , the Vindobona train also became categorised as EC 172/173 . The DR operations were taken over by Deutsche Bahn AG by 1994 . With the opening of Berlin Hauptbahnhof on 28 May 2008 , trains passed through Berlin via the new North–South mainline . As of 2010 , trains ran from Villach Hauptbahnhof to Hamburg-Altona via Klagenfurt , Vienna

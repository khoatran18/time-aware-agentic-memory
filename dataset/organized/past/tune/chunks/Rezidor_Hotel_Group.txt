Rezidor_Hotel_Group | Predecessors
 SAS Group , the flag carrier of Scandinavian countries , opened their first hotel in Copenhagen , Denmark in 1960 . The hotel , the SAS Royal Hotel , was the worlds first designer hotel , which was designed by Arne Jacobsen . The hotel was initially under the catering division of the group . The hospitality and catering division then became SAS Catering and Hotels . In 1982 , the hotels were spun off into a separate division . It became a subsidiary known as SAS International Hotels in 1985 .

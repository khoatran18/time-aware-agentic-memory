Meet the Press | Under Russert
On June 13 , 2008 , Russert died of a sudden coronary thrombosis ( caused by a cholesterol plaque rupture ) . Former NBC Nightly News anchor Tom Brokaw hosted a special edition of Meet the Press dedicated to the life of Russert on June 15 , 2011 , in which Russerts chair was left empty as a tribute .

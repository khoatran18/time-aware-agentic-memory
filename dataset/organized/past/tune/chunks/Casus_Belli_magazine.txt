Casus_Belli_magazine | History
The magazine ended publication in 1999 , but was taken up again by Arkana Press in 2000 , who continued publishing it until 2006 . Casus Belli Presse , which was a new publisher , was established in 2009 , and kept publishing the magazine on a monthly basis beginning in July 2010 , but ceased in 2011 after five issues .

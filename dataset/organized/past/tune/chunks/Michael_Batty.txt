Michael Batty | Memberships and Fellowships
 Learned Societies : He has been elected a Fellow of the Royal Society ( FRS ) in 2009 , a Fellow of the British Academy in 2001 , a Fellow of Academy of Social Sciences in 2001 and a Fellow of the Royal Society of Arts in 1982 . Professional Institutes : He has been a Member and now Fellow of the Royal Town Planning Institute since 1971 , and the Chartered Institute of Logistics and Transport since 1984 . He has held the status of Fellow of the Royal Geographical Society since 1972 .

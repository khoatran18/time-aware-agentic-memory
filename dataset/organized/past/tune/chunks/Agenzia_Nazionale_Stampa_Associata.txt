Agenzia Nazionale Stampa Associata | Recent events
 On 11 February 2017 , ANSA scooped media outlets throughout the world with its coverage of Pope Benedict XVIs resignation , due to correspondent Giovanna Chirris fluency in Latin , which enabled her to instantly translate the news when it was first announced . On 26 August 2014 was signed a five-year partnership agreement ( 2015–2020 ) between AP and ANSA for text , images and video .

Agenzia Nazionale Stampa Associata | Profile
 In addition to the news in Italian ANSA transmits its news in English , Spanish , German , Portuguese and Arabic . Since 1996 the ANSA was the first agency in Italy to spread news via SMS . From 1985 to 1994 as President of ANSA he was covered by journalist Giovanni Giovannini . From 1997 until 2009 , , a diplomat , served as president of ANSA . Since 2003 , the Ansa through AnsaMed provides a news service covering the countries of the Mediterranean basin .

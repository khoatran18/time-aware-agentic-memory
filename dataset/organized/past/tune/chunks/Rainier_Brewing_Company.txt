Rainier_Brewing_Company | The Prohibition era
 Kopp and Hemrich produced Rainier beer in Washington until 1918 , when the state of Washington enacted its own prohibition , 4 years before the 18th amendment enacted the nationwide prohibition . During this time they opened a brewery in San Francisco where they brewed Rainier beer until 1920 when the 18th amendment was ratified . The company survived prohibition by producing a variety of different nonalcoholic products .

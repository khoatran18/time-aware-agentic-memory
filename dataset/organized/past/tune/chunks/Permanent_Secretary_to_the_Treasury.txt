=== CHUNK /wiki/Permanent_Secretary_to_the_Treasury#0@past ===
Permanent_Secretary_to_the_Treasury | Permanent Secretary to the Treasury
 The UK Permanent Secretary to the Treasury is the most senior civil servant at HM Treasury . The post originated as that of Assistant Secretary to the Treasury in 1805 ; that office was given new duties and renamed in 1867 as a Permanent Secretaryship . The position is generally regarded as the second most influential in Her Majestys Civil Service ; John Gieve ( Permanent Secretary from 1998 to 2002 ) and Gus ODonnell ( 2002–2005 ) were Permanent Secretaries to the Treasury who then became Cabinet Secretary , the most influential post .

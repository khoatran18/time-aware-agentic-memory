Yugoslavia_Davis_Cup_team | Yugoslavia Davis Cup team
A team representing the Federal Republic of Yugoslavia returned to competing again from 1995 . From 2003 this country has been renamed to Serbia and Montenegro and Yugoslav participation in the Davis Cup continues . Following further splits in 2006 , several new teams were created for the relevant constituent parts :

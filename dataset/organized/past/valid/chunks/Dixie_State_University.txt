Dixie_State_University | Athletics
 The trailblazers have won 16 PacWest Championships , 5 PacWest Community Engagement Awards , and have appeared in 34 NCAA Division II tournaments . The mens basketball team appeared in the sweet sixteen in 2011 and the womens volleyball appeared in 2014 . The womens softball team has appeared in the College World Series three times and finished as runner up of the 2015 College World Series . The athletic department as a whole has completed 8,500 hours of community service since 2010 and held a grade point average of 2.97 during the 2015–2017 academic year .

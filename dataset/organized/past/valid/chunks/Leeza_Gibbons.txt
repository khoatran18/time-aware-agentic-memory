Leeza_Gibbons | Career
In 1983 Gibbons joined WCBS-TV in New York City , where she became a host on Two on the Town , a program modeled after PM Magazine ; her co-host was Robb Weller . The following year , they would both join Entertainment Tonight , with Gibbons as a reporter and co-anchor of the programs weekend edition . Concurrently hosted , Leeza is her own NBC/syndicated talk show , which has run since June 1993 .

Moudud_Ahmed | Moudud Ahmed
 Moudud Ahmed ( 24 May 1940 – 16 March 2021 ) was a Bangladeshi attorney and politician . He was a standing committee member of Bangladesh Nationalist Party . Ahmed was elected as a Jatiya Sangsad member total five times from Noakhali-1 and Noakhali-5 constituencies .

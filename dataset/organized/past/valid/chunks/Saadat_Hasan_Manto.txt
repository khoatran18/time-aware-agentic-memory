Saadat_Hasan_Manto | Saadat Hasan Manto
 Manto was tried for obscenity six times ; thrice before 1947 in British India , and thrice after independence in 1947 in Pakistan , but was never convicted . He is acknowledged as one of the finest 20th century Urdu writers and is the subject of two biographical films : Manto , directed by Sarmad Khoosat and the 2021 film Manto , directed by Nandita Das .

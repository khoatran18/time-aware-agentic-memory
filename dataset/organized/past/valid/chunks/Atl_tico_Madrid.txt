Atl_tico_Madrid | History
Athletics first ground , the Ronda de Vallecas , was in the eponymous working-class area on the south side of the city . In 1919 , the Compañía Urbanizadora Metropolitana—the company that ran the underground communication system in Madrid—acquired some land , near the Ciudad Universitaria . In 1921 , Athletic Madrid became independent of parent-club Athletic Bilbao and moved into a 35,800-seater stadium built by the company , the Estadio Metropolitano de Madrid . The Metropolitano continues to be used . After the move , the Metropolitano was demolished

Atléticos best years coincided with dominant Real Madrid teams . Between 1961 and 1980 , Real Madrid dominated La Liga , winning the competition 14 times . During this era , only Atlético offered Real any serious challenge , winning La Liga titles in 1967 , 1970 , 1973 and 1977 and finishing runners-up in 1961 , 1963 and 1965 .

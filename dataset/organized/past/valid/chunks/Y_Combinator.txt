Y_Combinator | History
 Y Combinator was founded in 2005 by Paul Graham , Jessica Livingston , Trevor Blackwell , and Robert Tappan Morris . Since 2005 , one program has been in Cambridge , Massachusetts , and one has been in Mountain View , California . As Y Combinator grew to 40 investments per year , running two programs became too much . In January 2009 , Y Combinator announced that the Cambridge program would be closed and all future programs would be in Silicon Valley .

Y_Combinator | Programs
- Nonprofit research lab YC Research was announced in October 2015 . Researchers are paid as full-time employees and can receive equity in Y Combinator . OpenAI was the first project undertaken by YC Research , and in January 2016 a second study on basic income was also announced . Another project is research on new cities . Australian quantum physicist Gerard Milburn is a research fellow at YC Research since 2017 .

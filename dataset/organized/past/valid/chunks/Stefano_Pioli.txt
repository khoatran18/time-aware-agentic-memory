Stefano_Pioli | Early career
 From 1999 to 2002 Pioli coached Bolognas youth team ( winning a championship with Allievi Nazionalis team ) , and Veronas in 2002–03 . In 2003–04 , he was appointed as head coach of Serie B club Salernitana . He moved to Modena , still of Serie B , in 2004 .

=== CHUNK /wiki/Stefano_Pioli#8@past ===
Stefano_Pioli | Palermo
 On 2 June 2008 , Pioli was named head coach of Serie A club Palermo , but he was relieved of his duties just 90 days later .

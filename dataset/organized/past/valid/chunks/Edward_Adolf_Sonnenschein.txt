Edward_Adolf_Sonnenschein | Career
 Sonnenschein was educated at University College School and then in 1868 at University College London . He was appointed Oxford professor of Greek and Latin at Mason College ( afterwards the University of Birmingham ) in 1883 , and remains there . He was a Plautine scholar , publishing editions of Captivi ( 1879 ) , Mostellaria ( 1884 ) , and Rudens ( 1891 ) . He took up the reform of grammar teaching , and published the Parallel Grammar series . With John Percival Postgate , he founded the Classical Association in 1903 .

Sonnenschein was an influential classical scholar during his time at Mason College between 1883 and 1918 , where he wrote prolifically . He edited several plays by Plautus , and collaborated with John Percival Postgate , forming the Classical Association in 1907 , becoming its Secretary . He contributed to the 1911 Encyclopædia Britannica ( designated by the initials E . A . So. ) .

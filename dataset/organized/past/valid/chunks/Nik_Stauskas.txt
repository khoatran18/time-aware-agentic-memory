Nik_Stauskas | Nik Stauskas
Nikolas Tomas Stauskas ( born October 7 , 1993 ) is a Canadian professional basketball player for the Raptors 905 of the NBA G League . A native of Mississauga , Ontario , Stauskas played two seasons of National Collegiate Athletic Association ( NCAA ) competition for the Michigan Wolverines ending with the 2013–14 team before declaring for the NBA draft . Stauskas was drafted eighth overall in the 2014 NBA draft by the Toronto Raptors , for which he began his NBA career .

Young_Girl_with_a_Flower_Basket | Background
Having enjoyed some early success in 1901 , Picasso remained a struggling artist by 1905 , living in poverty in Montmartre . The work was painted at the new studio that he took on the top floor of the dilapidated building at 13 rue Ravignan , which the poet Max Jacob termed Le Bateau-Lavoir . The Cirque Médrano was situated nearby at the foot of Montmartre , and Picasso was inspired by the harlequins and saltimbanques , clowns , jugglers and other acts , making the transition from his bleak Blue Period to his more optimistic Rose Period .

HMAS_Cairns_J183 | Royal Australian Navy
 From commissioning until 16 October 1942 , Cairns was stationed at Fremantle and served as a convoy escort , anti-submarine patrol vessel , and minesweeper . On 16 October , the corvette was reassigned to the British Eastern Fleet and ordered to Kilindini , Kenya , arriving on 14 November .

=== CHUNK /wiki/HMAS_Cairns_(J183)#9@past ===
HMAS_Cairns_J183 | Royal Netherlands Navy
 Following the end of World War II , all Admiralty-owned Bathurst class corvettes were disposed of . Cairns was paid off in Brisbane on 17 January 1946 . She was immediately recommissioned into the Royal Australian Navy ( RNLN ) and renamed HNLMS Ambon .

Lou_Scheper_Berkenkamp | Early life
 Lou Scheper-Berkenkamp was born in Wesel and was the daughter of Adalbert Berkenkamp ( 1868-1947 ) and his wife Laura Johanna Katharina Darmstädter ( 1872-1956 ) . She had two brothers Alfred ( 1896-1917 ) and Walter ( 1910-1994 ) . Her father and her uncle Heinrich , ran the paper and paper bag factory in Wesel that had been established by her grandfather Heinrich Berkenkamp in 1865 .

Expobank_CZ | History
 In March 2016 , Expobank CZ a.s . repurchased EAST Portfolio , s.r.o . in which it acquired a 100% stake from the German LBBW . In March 2017 Expobank CZ a.s . was the first Czech bank to enter the Serbian market , where it became the sole shareholder of Eurobank a.d . Beograd . Marfin Bank subsequently changed its business name to Expobank Beograd . In October 2018 , Expobank announced the opening of its Cryptocurrency trading and investing services .

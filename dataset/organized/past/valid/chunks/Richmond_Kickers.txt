Richmond_Kickers | History
The Kickers briefly sponsored two developmental teams of their own : Richmond Kickers Future and Richmond Kickers Destiny . Future competed in the mens USL Premier Development League from 2002 to 2008 , whereas the Destiny competed in the womens USL W-League from 2004 to 2009 . The Kickers entered a multi-year deal to become the USL Pro affiliate of D.C . United in 2013 , and continued that affiliation through 2018 . Loudoun United FC was launched as a fully owned-and-operated affiliate of D.C . United for the 2019 USL Championship season , presumably ending the Kickers affiliation tenure

Valladolid | Public transport
 Urban transit system was based on the Valladolid tram network from 1881 to 1933 . A public urban bus system started in 1928 , and has been managed by different private tenders since then . Today the public company AUVASA operates the network , with 22 regular lines and 5 late night lines .

Valladolid | Etymology
 There is no direct evidence for the origin of the modern name of Valladolid . It appears as Valledolit in the Primera Crónica General ; earlier attested variants include Valledolidi , Valleolide ( 1092 ) and Valleolit , Valleoleti , Valleoliti ( 1095 ) .

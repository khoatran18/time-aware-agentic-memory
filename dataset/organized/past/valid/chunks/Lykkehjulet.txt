The show originally used the shopping format of the American program in which winnings were used to buy studios prizes , such as furniture , appliances , and trips . In later years the format was changed to reflect the American version of all-cash winnings . The host during the first season was Michael Meyerheim with Pia Dresner as the letter-turning co-host . Thereafter , the show has been hosted by Bengt Burg since 1989 , with a short stint by Keld Heick during the 1996-1997 season . The final season in 2001 season was hosted by Lars Herlow

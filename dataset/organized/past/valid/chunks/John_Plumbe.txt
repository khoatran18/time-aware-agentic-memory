John_Plumbe | Publishing
 In 1846 he founded the National Publishing Company which produced the weekly Popular Magazine ( Augustine J.H . Duganne , editor ) and other works . California and Iowa , 1849-1857 . Plumbe lived in California from 1849 to 1854 . In 1854 returned to Dubuque , Iowa . He may have worked for photographer Edward Anthony , c . 1855-1857 . He died in Iowa in 1857 , at age 48 .

ACES_Colombia | History
 In 1981 , it acquired its first Boeing 727-100 . In 1986 , expansion commenced with the purchase of additional 727-100 and the introduction of Fairchild FH-227 aircraft constructed under license by Fairchild in the United States and featuring slight modifications . The 727s had a capacity of 129 passengers and the Fairchilds of 44 . At this time the first international charter flights also began to Havana , Varadero , Nassau , Freeport , Montego Bay , Puerto Plata , Punta Cana , St . Kitts , St . Maarten , Porlamar and Cancun .

ACES_Colombia | History
In 1991 , ACES completely replaced its Fokker F27 Friendship fleet with ATR 42 turbo-prop aircraft for its short-haul regional routes , and its Boeing 727-100 with the Boeing 727-200 Advanced variant . In 1992 , ACES expanded its service internationally , operating flights from Medellín and Bogotá to Miami . Soon after , Juan Carlos Posada was appointed CEO and would serve until the airlines demise in 2003 .

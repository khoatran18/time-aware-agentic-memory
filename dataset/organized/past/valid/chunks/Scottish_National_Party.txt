Scottish_National_Party | History
The SNP was formed in 1931 through the merger of the National Party of Scotland and the Scottish Party , with the Duke of Montrose and Cunninghame Graham as its first , joint , presidents . Sir Alexander MacEwen was its first chairman . Professor Douglas Young , who was the leader of the Scottish National Party from 1942 to 1945 campaigned for the Scottish people to refuse conscription and his activities were popularly vilified as undermining the British war effort against the Axis powers . Young was imprisoned for refusing to be conscripted .

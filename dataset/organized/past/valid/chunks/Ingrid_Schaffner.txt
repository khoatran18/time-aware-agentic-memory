Ingrid_Schaffner | Ingrid Schaffner
 Ingrid Schaffner ( born 1958 ) is a curator , writer , and educator specializing in contemporary art since the mid-1980s . Schaffner work often coalesces around themes of archiving and collecting , photography , feminism , and alternate modernisms—especially Surrealism .

Carrier_Air_Wing_Seven | Cold War
After moving from Quonset Point to Naval Air Station Oceana in 1958 , the Air Group teamed up with and deployed as the first all jet air wing . On 20 December 1963 all Carrier Air Groups were re-designated as Carrier Air Wings and CVG-7 was renamed Carrier Air Wing Seven ( CVW-7 ) . During the years from 1966 – 1977 the air wing completed seven Mediterranean deployments in USS Independence and participated in numerous North Atlantic Treaty Organization ( NATO ) exercises .

Attaphol is and was next to Charnwit Polcheewin the only coach who managed a club from Thailand to lead to the final of the AFC Champions League . 2005-03 and 2003-04 he won with the club also two runner-up . In his team , which reached the final of the Champions League , were a number of exceptional players like Therdsak Chaiman , Worrawoot Srimaka , Dusit Chalermsan and Anurak Srikerd .

The_Clitheroe_Kid | Cast
Oldham comedian Danny Ross played Alfie Hall , Susans half-witted , tongue-tied boyfriend , who was often drawn into Jimmys reckless schemes . He joined the show in 1964 , replacing Susans original boyfriend , played by Peter Goodwright .

=== CHUNK /wiki/The_Clitheroe_Kid#11@past ===
The_Clitheroe_Kid | Production
Created by James Casey in 1956 , the show was produced in Manchester , originally by the North Region studios of the BBC Home Service . After two series had been broadcast on North Region only , in 1959 the show was judged popular enough to be transferred to the national transmitters of the BBC Light Programme .

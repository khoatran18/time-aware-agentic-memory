Such doctrinal differences , plus Duncans insistence on total control over his parishioners lives , led to a split with the Church of England . Duncan was expelled from the SPG in 1881 and transformed his mission into a nondenominational Independent Native Church . Eventually , he decided to found a second utopian community on Annette Island , Alaska , on the territory of the Tongass tribe of Tlingit . He obtained permission from the US government—travelling to testify before Congress himself—to establish an Indian reservation there ( still Alaskas sole Indian reservation ) , then led approximately 800 Tsimshians

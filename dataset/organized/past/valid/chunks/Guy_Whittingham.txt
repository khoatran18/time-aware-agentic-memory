Guy_Whittingham | Playing career
 In the 2000–01 season , his last as a professional , he scored for three clubs , notching for Peterborough United ( against Oldham Athletic ) , Oxford United ( against Swindon Town ) and Wycombe Wanderers ( against Bristol City ) . He also featured in Wycombes FA Cup semi-final against Liverpool . It was the second occasion on which Whittingham had been denied in an FA Cup semi-final by the Merseyside team , after losing a penalty shootout to Liverpool while at Portsmouth in 1992 .

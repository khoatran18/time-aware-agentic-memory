Conrad_Theodor_van_Deventer | Early career
On 20 August 1880 he was made available to the Governor-General of the Dutch East Indies by the Ministry of Colonies to be appointed as civil service official . With his wife Van Deventer travelled in September 1880 to Surabaya by steamboat Prins Hendrik ; he was appointed court clerk at the Raad van Justitie ( Council of Justice ) at Amboina in December 1880 . As early as 1881 Van Deventer was already seen by the public as an authority in the case of the issue of the economic position of the Dutch East Indies in relation to motherland

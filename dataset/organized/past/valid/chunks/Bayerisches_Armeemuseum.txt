Bayerisches_Armeemuseum | Museum in Munich
 During the Second World War , the building was partially destroyed . The preserved dome of the old museum building in Munich is the central building of the newly established Bayerische Staatskanzlei ( state chancellery ) today . Since 1946 , the Armeemuseum has been housed in the Bavarian National Museum in Munich .

 Researchers of the museum are : - Ansgar Reiß , Museum director - Dieter Storz , main conservator - Thomas Müller , curator - Tobias Schönauer , conservator - Daniel Hohrath , curator ( Army Library ) - Frank Wernitz , curator In the 1970s , Rotraud Werde was a designated expert on uniforms at the museum . From 1979 to 2011 , Jürgen Weber was curator and main conservator at the museum .

Writers_Guild_of_America_West | Governance
 David Young is employed as the Guilds executive director and Tony Segall is general counsel . Segall served as the Guilds chief negotiator during the 2007 contract negotiations and subsequent 100-day strike . According to WGAws Department of Labor records since 2006 , over half of the guilds total membership is ineligible to vote , comprising the guilds post current , emeritus , and associate members .

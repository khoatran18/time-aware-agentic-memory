Steven_Ozment | Steven Ozment
 Steven Edgar Ozment ( February 21 , 1939 – December 12 , 2019 ) was an American historian of early modern and modern Germany , the European family , and the Protestant Reformation . From 1990 to 2015 , he served as the McLean Professor of Ancient and Modern History at Harvard University , and was Professor Emeritus until his death on December 12 , 2019 .

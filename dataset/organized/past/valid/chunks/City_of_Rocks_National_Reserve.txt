City_of_Rocks_National_Reserve | Native American history
In 1826 , Peter Skene Ogden and his Snake River brigade of beaver trappers were the first Euro-Americans to note the City of Rocks . Having few beaver , the area remains ignored , while growing streams of wagons have begun flowing through the area in the summer .

City_of_Rocks_National_Reserve | Facts and designations
The Idaho Legislature declared Section 36 within City of Rocks as a state park under the jurisdiction of the Idaho Land Board on February 27 , 1957 . In 1964 , a significantly larger area ( more than ) was named a National Historic Landmark . On March 15 , 1973 , Section 36 was transferred to the Idaho Department of Parks and Recreation from the Department of Lands . The following year , the area was designated a National Natural Landmark . The National Park Service studied the site in the 1970s and 80s as a proposed national monument

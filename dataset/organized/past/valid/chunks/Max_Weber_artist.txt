Max_Weber_artist | Max Weber ( artist )
 Max Weber ( April 18 , 1881 – October 4 , 1961 ) was a Jewish-American painter and one of the first American Cubist painters who , in later life , turned to more figurative Jewish themes in his art . He is best known today for Chinese Restaurant ( 1914 ) , in the collection of the Whitney Museum of American Art , the finest canvas of his Cubist phase , in the words of art historian Avis Berman .

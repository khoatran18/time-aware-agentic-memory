British_Rail_Class_15 | Preservation
One of the former train heating units , D8233 , was purchased for preservation in 1984 and is now the only survivor of the type . It was originally kept at the South Yorkshire Railway in Sheffield , moving in 1986 to the East Lancashire Railway , and in 1988 to the Mangapps Farm Railway , where it remains . D8233 then moved to Crewe following an agreement with the Waterman Heritage Trust . Since its initial preservation the locomotive had received little work apart from cosmetic attention . British_Rail_Class_15 | British Rail Class 15
The Class 15 was ordered by British Railways ( BR ) shortly after the announcement of the 1955 Modernisation Plan , which led to the procurement of a diverse number of diesel locomotives under the pilot scheme . Soon after the first locomotive was completed during 1957 , its performance proved sufficient to warrant multiple follow-on orders , resulting in a total fleet of 44 locomotives .

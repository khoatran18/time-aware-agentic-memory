David Wilson Jolly ( born October 31 , 1972 ) is an American attorney , former lobbyist , and politician who held the office of U.S . Representative for Floridas 13th congressional district , based in Pinellas County , from 2014 to 2017 . A former Republican , he previously served as general counsel to his predecessor , Bill Young . He won the race for Youngs seat in a special election against Democrat Alex Sink .

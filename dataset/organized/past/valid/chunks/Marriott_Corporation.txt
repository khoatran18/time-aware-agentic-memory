Marriott_Corporation | History
In 1976 , Marriott opened two theme parks called Marriotts Great America in California and Illinois . Another was planned for in Maryland but local opposition prevented construction from ever beginning at any of the three proposed sites . The parks had replicas of the first Hot Shoppes . Both parks were sold during the mid-1980s , with the California park sold to the city of Santa Clara , California and the Illinois park sold to Six Flags in 1984 . They are both renamed Californias Great America and Six Flags Great America , respectively .

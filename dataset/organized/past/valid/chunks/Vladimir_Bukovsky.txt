 Vladimir Konstantinovich Bukovsky ( ; ; 30 December 1942 – 27 October 2022 ) was a Russian-born British human rights activist and writer . From the late 1950s to the mid-1970s , he was a prominent figure in the Soviet dissident movement , well known at home and abroad . He spent a total of twelve years in the psychiatric prison-hospitals , labour camps , and prisons of the Soviet Union .

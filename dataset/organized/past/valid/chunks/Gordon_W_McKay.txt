Gordon_W_McKay | Early life
 McKay was born on March 17 , 1912 in Aitkin , Minnesota . His parents were Clyde Marshall and Olive Elizabeth ( Spencer ) McKay . In 1911 , when he was less than a year old , McKay moved with his parents to Oregon . They traveled to central Oregon by passenger train , disembarking at Shaniko , where the railroad line ended at that time . From there the family , took a stagecoach to Bend , where they settled .

Jeff_Goldblum | Personal life
 From 1980 to 1985 , Goldblum was married to Patricia Gaul , with whom he appeared in Silverado ( 1985 ) . Since 1987 , he has been married to Geena Davis , his co-star in Transylvania 6-5000 ( 1985 ) , The Fly ( 1986 ) , and Earth Girls Are Easy ( 1988 ) .

Jeff_Goldblum | Career
 In November 2015 , he joined the ensemble cast of Shane Carruths third film The Modern Ocean . Goldblum played The Grandmaster in the Marvel superhero film ( 2017 ) . He reprised his role as Dr . Ian Malcolm in the franchises fifth film ( 2020 ) and will do so again , this time with fellow Jurassic Park alumni Sam Neill and Laura Dern , in , currently filming .

Lega_Nord_Piemont | Splits and recovery
Troubled by splits and a huge loss of popular support ( the party was reduced from 18.2% to 7.8% in just three years ) , LNP entered into the centre-right House of Freedoms coalition . Since 2000 the party has taken part to the regional government led by Enzo Ghigo ( Forza Italia ) , which included LNP long-time leader Gipo Farassino as regional minister of Culture , while Roberto Cota was appointed President of the Regional Council .

Lega_Nord_Piemont | Splits and recovery
 In 2002 the party suffered a damaging split when Comino left the party over disagreements with Umberto Bossi , federal secretary of Lega Nord , and started his own party , which was integrated into the Autonomists for Europe ( ApE ) in 2000 .

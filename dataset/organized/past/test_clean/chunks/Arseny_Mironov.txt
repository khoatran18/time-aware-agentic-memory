Arseny_Mironov | Arseny Mironov
 Arseny Dmitrievich Mironov ( 25 December 1917 – 3 July 2019 ) was a Russian scientist , aerospace engineer , and aviator . He is one of the oldest researchers in aircraft aerodynamics and flight testing , a Gromov Flight Research Institute ( GFRI ) director since 1981 , a recipient of the Stalin Prize in 1948 and the USSR State Prize in 1976 , and an honorary citizen of Zhukovsky .

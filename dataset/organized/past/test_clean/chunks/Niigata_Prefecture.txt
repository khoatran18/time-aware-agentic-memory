Niigata_Prefecture | Mining and manufacturing
 Crude oil is produced in Niigata Prefecture , although Japan relies heavily on petroleum imported from other countries . Kerosene heaters are also produced for use in the cold Niigata winters . Kinzan , on Sado Island , is an active gold mine . Sanjō and Tsubame produce 90 percent of all the silverware made in Japan . The two cities are second after Osaka in the production of scissors , kitchen knives , and wrenches .

Niigata_Prefecture | History
 On October 23 , 2004 , the Chūetsu earthquake struck Niigata Prefecture and was measured at Shindo 6+ at Ojiya . On January 9 , 2006 , a heavy winter storm struck the prefecture and its neighbors . At least 71 people died and more than 1,000 were injured . Also in 2006 , a massive tsunami and earthquake damaged homes and caused casualties in the maritime areas of Niigata Prefecture , especially near Sado Island . On July 16 , 2007 , another earthquake struck the region .

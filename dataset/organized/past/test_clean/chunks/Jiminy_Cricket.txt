Jiminy_Cricket | At theme parks
In the 1950s–70s , Jiminy Cricket appeared in four series of educational films aimed at grade-school audiences . In the Im No Fool series , he advised children how to steer clear of dangerous traffic , sharp objects , strangers , exposed electrical lines , and so forth . Several of those series have been first shown on The Mickey Mouse Club since 1955 .

Jiminy_Cricket | Voice actors
Jiminy Cricket has been voiced in English by six actors . He was originally performed by singer Cliff Edwards , who voiced the character for Disney through the 1960s and sang Jiminys most famous song , When You Wish Upon a Star . Following Edwards death , Clarence Nash briefly voiced him , until 1973 , when Eddie Carroll assumed the role as Jiminys voice actor . Carroll played the role for 37 years , until his death in 2010 . Actor Hal Smith voiced Jiminy in the Pinocchio Read-Along Storybook in 1992 .

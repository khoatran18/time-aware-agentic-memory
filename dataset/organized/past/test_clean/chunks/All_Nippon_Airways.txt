NH began helicopter services in February 1953 . On 15 December 1953 , it operated its first cargo flight between Osaka and Tokyo using a de Havilland Dove , JA5008 . This was the first scheduled flight flown by a Japanese pilot in postwar Japan . Passenger service on that route commenced on 1 February 1954 , and was upgraded to a de Havilland Heron in March .

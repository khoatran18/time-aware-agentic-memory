Russia gained control of Izmail again after the Russo-Turkish War of 1877–1878 . Amid the dissolution of the Russian Empire in 1917 and in the aftermath of World War I , the city was occupied by the Romanian Army on 22 January 1918 , after a skirmish with troops of the Danube flotilla .

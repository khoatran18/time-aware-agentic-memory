British_Vogue | History
 Under Audrey Withers ( editor from 1940 to 1960 ) , the magazine again took a literary direction , and during the Second World War it even took part in reporting the war . In 1944 , the American photographer Lee Miller convinced Withers to dispatch her to Normandy to create an article about wartime nursing ; Miller then accompanied the Allied advance through Europe , covering the liberation of Paris and sending a story from Buchenwald . Dame Anna Wintour edited the British edition from 1985 to 1987 , before taking over Vogue in New York City .

British_Vogue | British Vogue
 British Vogue is a magazine whose success is based upon its advertising rather than its sales revenue . In 2003 , it ran 2,020 pages of advertising at an average of £16,000 a page . It is deemed to be more commercial than other editions of Vogue . British Vogue is the most profitable British magazine as well as the most profitable edition of Vogue besides the US and China editions .

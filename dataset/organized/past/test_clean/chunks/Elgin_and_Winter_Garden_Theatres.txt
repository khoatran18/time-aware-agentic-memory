Elgin_and_Winter_Garden_Theatres | Recent history
 Since 1980 , the Elgin Theatre has served as one of the hosts to the annual Toronto International Film Festival . In 1981 , the Ontario Heritage Foundation bought the structure from Famous Players . From March 1985 through March 1987 the musical Cats was very successfully presented in the essentially unrestored Elgin , showing the viability of the theatre . The building closed in 1987 for a full restoration and reopened in 1989 .

 The Elgin Theatre housed the world premiere of the Napoleon musical in 1994 , which later transferred to Londons West End in 2000 . In 1995 , it was home to The Whos Tommy musical . From 1996 onward , Ross Petty Productions has presented pantomimes at the Elgin Theatre every Christmas season .

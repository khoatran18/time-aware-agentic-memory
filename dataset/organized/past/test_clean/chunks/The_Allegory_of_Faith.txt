The_Allegory_of_Faith | The Allegory of Faith
 The Allegory of Faith , also called Allegory of the Catholic Faith , is a painting of the Dutch Golden Age by Johannes Vermeer dating from about 1670–1672 . It has been in the Metropolitan Museum of Art in New York since 1931 .

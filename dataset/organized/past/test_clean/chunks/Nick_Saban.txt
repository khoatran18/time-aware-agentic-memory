Nick_Saban | Early coaching career
 Saban had not intended to enter the coaching ranks until Don James hired him as a graduate assistant at Kent State , while Saban waited for his wife to graduate . He has served as an assistant coach in NCAA Division I-A , at several schools : Syracuse in 1977 , West Virginia in 1978 and 1979 , Ohio State in 1980 and 1981 , Navy in 1982 , and Michigan State since 1983 .

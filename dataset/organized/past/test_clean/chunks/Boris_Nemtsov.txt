Boris_Nemtsov | Early life
Nemtsov was among the 34 original signatories of the online anti-Putin manifesto Putin must go , published on 10 March 2010 . Six months later , in September 2010 , together with Vladimir Ryzhkov , Mikhail Kasyanov and Vladimir Milov , Nemtsov formed the For Russia without Lawlessness and Corruption party , which , three months later was transformed into the Peoples Freedom Party . In May 2011 , the party submitted an application for registration to the Ministry of Internal Affairs , but one month later it was denied .

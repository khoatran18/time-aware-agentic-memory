Audie_Murphy | European Theater
 For his actions that day , he was awarded the Medal of Honor . The 3rd Infantry Division was awarded the Presidential Unit Citation for its actions at the Colmar Pocket , giving Murphy a Bronze Oak Leaf Cluster for the emblem . On 16 February , Murphy was promoted to first lieutenant and has been awarded the Legion of Merit for his service from 22 January 1944 to the present . He was moved from the front lines to Regimental Headquarters and made a liaison officer .

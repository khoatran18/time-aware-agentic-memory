Auditorium_Building_Chicago | Later uses
 On October 31 , 1967 , the Auditorium Theatre reopened and through 1975 , the Auditorium served as a rock venue . Among other notable acts , the Grateful Dead has played there ten times since 1971 . The Doors also played their first concert at the Auditorium Building after their arrest of singer Jim Morrison on June 14 , 1969 . It was declared a National Historic Landmark by the U.S . Department of the Interior in 1975 .

=== CHUNK /wiki/Auditorium_Building_(Chicago)#2@past ===
Auditorium_Building_Chicago | Auditorium Building ( Chicago )
 The building was added to the National Register of Historic Places on April 17 , 1970 . It was declared a National Historic Landmark in 1975 , and was designated a Chicago Landmark on September 15 , 1976 . In addition , it is a historic district contributing property for the Chicago Landmark Historic Michigan Boulevard District . Since 1951 , the Auditorium Building has been part of Roosevelt University .

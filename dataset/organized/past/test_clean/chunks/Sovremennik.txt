Sovremennik | Sovremennik
 When Pushkin died , his friend Pyotr Pletnyov took over the editorship in 1838 . A few years later the magazine fell into decline , and Pletnyov handed it over to Nikolay Chernyshevsky and Ivan Panaev in 1847 . It was Nekrasov who really made the magazine profitable . He enlisted the services of Ivan Turgenev , Ivan Goncharov , Alexander Herzen and Nikolai Ogaryov . Sovremennik was the first to publish translated works by Charles Dickens , George Sand and other best-selling foreign writers .

Michael_Cullen_politician | Fourth Labour Government
When Labour came to government in 1984 , Cullen took on the position of Senior Whip . Due to his knowledge of economics , Cullen became increasingly involved in the disputes surrounding the Minister of Finance , Roger Douglas , who supported the liberalisation of trade and the sale of state assets plus deep tax cuts . These goals , which were against traditional Labour policies , angered both party members and the public .

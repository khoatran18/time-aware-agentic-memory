Gerard_Bilders | Biography
 Bilders was born in Utrecht , where he lived until 1856 , though from 1838 to 1845 the Bilders family lived in Oosterbeek , a village near Arnhem which later became a major center for Dutch plein-air-painters . His father Johannes Warnardus Bilders was a Dutch landscape-painter . In 1857 Gerard moved to The Hague .

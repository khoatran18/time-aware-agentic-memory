HMAS_Wollongong_J172 | RNLN service
 After the end of the war , Wollongong was marked for transfer to the Royal Netherlands Navy ( RNLN ) . She was decommissioned in Sydney on 11 February 1946 , was recommissioned into the RNLN , and renamed HNLMS Banda . The corvette remained with the RAN until April 1950 .

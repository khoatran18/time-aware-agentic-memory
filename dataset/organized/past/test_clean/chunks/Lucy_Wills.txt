lecturing in botany at the South African College , then part of the University of the Cape of Good Hope . She and Wills were both interested in Sigmund Freuds theories . At the outbreak of war in August 1914 , Gordon enlisted in the Royal Scots Fusiliers . Wills spent some weeks doing voluntary nursing in a hospital in Cape Town , before she and Margot Hume returned to England , arriving in Plymouth in December .

Peter_Beattie | Peter Beattie
 Peter Douglas Beattie ( born 18 November 1952 ) is a former Australian politician who served as the 36th Premier of Queensland , in office from 1998 to 2007 . He was the state leader of the Liberal Party from 1996 to 2007 .

Peter_Beattie | Autobiography
In May 2008 Beattie released his autobiography Making A Difference , in which he described his upbringing , political life and his views on key issues , including health , education and social reform . The book is part memoir , part manifesto . Beattie says that the reason he released the book while he is in office , rather than when he is retired , is because no-one would want to read about him if he was not in the public arena .

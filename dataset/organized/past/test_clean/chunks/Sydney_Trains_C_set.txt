Sydney_Trains_C_set | In service
 The first set entered service in July 1986 , operating out of Flemington Maintenance Depot , some were delivered to Hornsby Maintenance Depot , but by December 1988 , all were operating out of Punchbowl Maintenance Depot as sets K40-K51 .

=== CHUNK /wiki/Sydney_Trains_C_set#4@past ===
Sydney_Trains_C_set | Design & construction
The C set was distinguished externally by a fibreglass molding mounted on the front of the power carriage . It was originally in the State Rail Authority white with orange and red candy livery stripes on the lower portion . C3596 was the only power car to have its Candy-liveried front repainted in CityRail blue and yellow prior to its Citydecker refurbishment in 2000 . The chopper cars were amongst the heaviest in the suburban fleet , with the power cars weighing 52 tonnes .

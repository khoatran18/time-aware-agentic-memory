Guy_Aldred | Agnosticism
Guy became a speaker at the Institute of Theism , but soon felt it was time to set up his own organisation . In 1904 he founded the Theistic Church , which met every Sunday . With a considerable , though sometimes boisterous , crowd , Guy was gaining a reputation as a forceful young orator . He was also shifting towards atheism . By August , the meeting banner was changed to read The Clerkenwell Freethought Mission . Meetings often generated extreme hostility .

Pierre_Gasly | Karting
 Gasly entered competitive karting in 2006 at the age of ten , when he finished fifteenth in the French Minime Championship , before he finished fourth the following year . In 2008 he stepped up to the French Cadet Championship , before moving to the international scene in 2009 . He has moved into the KF3 category , is staying , and he finished as runner-up in the CIK-FIA European Championship .

Pierre_Gasly | Japanese Super Formula
 Gasly joined Team TOM'S to drive a Red Bull-sponsored Honda at the 2017 Super Formula Championship , winning two races .

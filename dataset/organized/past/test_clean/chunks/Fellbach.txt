Fellbach | History
Fellbach was first mentioned as Velbach in 1121 . The name Velebach appeared in 1357 and Fehlbach in 1800 . Several landlords had possessions in Fellbach , the House Württemberg bought piece by piece . First , the village belonged to Oberamt Cannstatt . After its dissolution in 1927 Fellbach came to Oberamt Waiblingen , this became in 1938 Waiblingen district . After Fellbach had grown to the largest Württemberg village , the community was named October 14 , 1933 for city . After World War II , the population crossed the 20,000 threshold .

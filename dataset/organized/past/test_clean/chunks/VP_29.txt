VP_29 | Operational history
 - 8 June 1944 : VP-72 was consolidated at Ebeye Island , with all aircraft and crews berthed ashore . Maintenance and overhaul of the remaining squadron aircraft were conducted to prepare them for the return to NAS Kaneohe Bay when relieved . - 17 July 1944 : VP-72 was relieved by VP-18 for return to NAS Kaneohe Bay and eventual transfer back to the continental United States . - 1 August 1944 : The squadron reached San Diego and all hands were granted 30-day rehabilitation leave .

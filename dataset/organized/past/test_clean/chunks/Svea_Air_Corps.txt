Svea_Air_Corps | History
 The meadows in the area were used since 1913 for basic flying training . From 1919 until 1936 the airport was a permanent international airport until the commercial traffic was transferred to the newly built Stockholm-Bromma Airport with paved runways . From 1926 until 1938 the airfield was also used for evaluation of new aircraft for the Swedish Navy . Units from other wings were also stationed here for air defense of the capital Stockholm .

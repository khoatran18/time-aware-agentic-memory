Oliver_Bulleid | Recognition and retirement
 Bulleid was elected president of the Institution of Mechanical Engineers for 1946 . He was also president of the Institution of Locomotive Engineers and of the Institute of Welding , and was elected to the Smeatonian Society of Civil Engineers . He was appointed CBE in the 1949 New Year Honours . Bulleid is still working , moving to Belstone in Devon , then Exmouth . He was awarded an Honorary Doctorate of Science by the University of Bath in 1967 . Shortly after , he moved to Malta , where he died in 1970 aged 87 .

Oliver_Bulleid | Oliver Bulleid
He was born in Invercargill , New Zealand , to William Bulleid and his wife Marian Pugh , both British immigrants . After his father died in 1889 , his mother went back to Llanfyllin , Wales , where the family home had been , taking Bulleid with her . In 1901 , after a technical education at Accrington Grammar School , he joined the Great Northern Railway ( GNR ) at Doncaster at the age of 18 , as an apprentice under H . A .

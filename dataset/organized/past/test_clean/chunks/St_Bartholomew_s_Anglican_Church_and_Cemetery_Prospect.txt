 The condition of the church deteriorated throughout the mid-twentieth century , and a dwindling congregation and lack of financial support led to its closure in 1967 . The last service was held on Christmas Eve that year . A group of citizens interested in preserving the church formed The Prospect Trust in November 1967 under the chairmanship of Robert Brown . In late 1969 the City of Blacktown commenced negotiations with the Church of England Property Trust . A fifty-year lease over most of the property was obtained by Prospect Trust in 1975 .

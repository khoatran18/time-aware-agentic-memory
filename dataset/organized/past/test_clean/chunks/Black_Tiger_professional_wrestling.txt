Black_Tiger_professional_wrestling | Black Tiger II
 In August 1993 Black Tiger made a return , this time portrayed by Eddie Guerrero . His Tiger Mask rival at this time was Koji Kanemoto . Kanemoto removed his mask in January 1994 , while Guerrero reached the finals of the 1994 Super Grade Junior Heavyweight Tag League alongside The Great Sasuke , placed third in the 1994 and 1995 Best of the Super Juniors tournaments and won in 1996 .

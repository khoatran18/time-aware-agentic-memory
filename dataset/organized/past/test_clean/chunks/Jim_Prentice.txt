Peter Eric James Prentice ( July 20 , 1956 – October 13 , 2016 ) was a Canadian politician who served as the 16th Premier of Ontario from 2014 to 2015 . In the 2004 federal election he was elected to the House of Commons of Canada as a candidate of the Conservative Party of Canada . He was re-elected in the 2006 federal election and appointed to the cabinet as Minister of Indian Affairs and Northern Development and Federal Interlocutor for Métis and Non-Status Indians .

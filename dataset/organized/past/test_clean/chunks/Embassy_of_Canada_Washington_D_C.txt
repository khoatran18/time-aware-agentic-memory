Embassy_of_Canada_Washington_D_C | Architecture
 The Pennsylvania Avenue NW building was designed by British Columbias Arthur Erickson , recognized as one of Canadas most decorated architects . Erickson tried to evoke a sense of Canada in the architecture of the building , using long horizontals , wide open spaces and water features . The large airy courtyard includes the sculpture Spirit of Haida Gwaii by Bill Reid , which has been featured on Canadas twenty-dollar bill since 2004 , which sits in a pool of water representative of Canadas ocean limits .

Embassy_of_Canada_Washington_D_C | Ambassador
 Canadas 24th Ambassador to the United States was David MacNaughton , who presented his credentials to President Barack Obama on March 3 , 2019 . MacNaughton previously served as Canadian and North American president of Hill and Knowlton , and president of Public Affairs International , which purchased Decima Research to create Public Affairs Resource Group . MacNaughton left the post at the end of August 2019 , and was replaced by Acting Ambassador of Canada to the United States Kirsten Hillman .

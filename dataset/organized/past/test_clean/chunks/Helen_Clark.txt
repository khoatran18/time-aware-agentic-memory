=== CHUNK /wiki/Helen_Clark#5@past ===
Helen_Clark | Helen Clark
 Clark resigned from Parliament in April 2009 shortly after losing the 2008 general election , to become the first female head of the United Nations Development Programme ( UNDP ) . In 2016 , she stood for the position of Secretary-General of the Commonwealth of Nations , but was unsuccessful . She left her UNDP administrator post on 19 April 2017 at the end of her second four-year term and was succeeded by Achim Steiner . In 2019 , Clark became the patron of The Helen Clark Foundation .

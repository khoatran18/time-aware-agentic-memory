DRB_Class_50 | DRB Class 50
This class was procured as part of the German Nazi partys preparations for war that led into the Second World War . Up to now , 3,164 Class 50 engines have been built by almost all the European locomotive factories – as so-called provisional war locomotives ( Übergangskriegslokomotiven ) and classified as 50 ÜK .

=== CHUNK /wiki/DRB_Class_50#29@past ===
DRB_Class_50 | Other Railway Companies
 After the Second World War several Class 50 locomotives were left in eastern Europe , where some of the railway administrations procured more of them . For example , 282 copies of Class 50 locomotives were produced by the Romanian State Railway CFR between 1947 and 1959 . 40 Romanian 150s supplied to China in 1958 , they became class DK5 in 1959 and numbered 241–280 . DK5-250 is preserved at the Shenyang Railway Museum . Some were also supplied to the Korean State Railway in North Korea , where they were numbered in the 150 series .

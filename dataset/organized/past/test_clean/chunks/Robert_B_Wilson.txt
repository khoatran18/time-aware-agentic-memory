Robert_B_Wilson | Memberships and awards
 He is an elected member of the National Academy of Sciences , a designated distinguished fellow of the American Economic Association , and a fellow , former officer and Council member of the Econometric Society . He was conferred an honorary Doctor of Economics degree in 1986 by the Norwegian School of Economics and Business Administration . In 1995 , the University of Chicago conferred on him an honorary Doctor of Laws degree . In 2014 , Wilson won a Golden Goose Award for his work involving auction design .

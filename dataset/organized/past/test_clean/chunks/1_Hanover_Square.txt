The brownstone initially served as the headquarters of the Hanover Bank , while other commercial tenants occupied the brick buildings . The New York Cotton Exchange , founded in 1870 , has occupied the building since 1872 . The building subsequently served as the headquarters of W.R . Grace and Company until the early 1910s . In 1914 , the structures were purchased by the India House , a private club for gentlemen involved in foreign commerce , which continues to occupy the building .

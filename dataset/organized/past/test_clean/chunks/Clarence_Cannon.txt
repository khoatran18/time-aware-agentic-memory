Clarence_Cannon | Clarence Cannon
 Clarence Andrew Cannon ( April 11 , 1879 – May 12 , 1964 ) was a Democratic Congressman from Kansas serving from 1923 until his death in Washington , D.C . in 1964 . He was a notable parliamentarian and chaired the U.S . House Committee on Appropriations . He is the longest-serving member of the United States House of Representatives from the state of Missouri .

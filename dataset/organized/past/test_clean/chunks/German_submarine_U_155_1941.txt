German_submarine_U_155_1941 | German submarine U-155 ( 1941 )
 German submarine U-155 was a Type IXC U-boat of Nazi Germanys Kriegsmarine built for service during World War II . Her keel was laid down on 1 October 1944 by DeSchiMAG AG Weser in Bremen as yard number 997 . She was launched on 12 May 1941 and commissioned on 23 August with Kapitänleutnant Adolf Piening in command . Piening was relieved in February 1944 ( after being promoted to Korvettenkapitän ) , by Oberleutnant zur See Johannes Rudolph .

=== CHUNK /wiki/German_submarine_U-155_(1941)#19@past ===
German_submarine_U_155_1941 | Fate
 On 30 June 1945 , following the German surrender , she was moved from Wilhelmshaven to Loch Ryan , Scotland for Royal Navy Operation Deadlight , the scuttling of surrendered German U-boats , and was sunk on 21 December the same year .

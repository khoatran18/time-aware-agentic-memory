Andr_Schembri | German stint
 After his contract expired with Marsaxlokk , Schembri joined Austria Kärnten on a two-year deal on 19 June 2009 . Amid the clubs financial problems and a disappointing start to the season which saw them sitting at the bottom of the table , Schembri currently remains one of few players who are still with the club . His only goal for the Austrian club came in a 2–3 defeat against Kapfenberger SV on 26 July .

Andr_Schembri | Apollon Limassol
On 13 June 2017 , Schembri signed with Apollon Limassol , returning to Cyprus after his earlier stint with AEL Limassol . In his first game played on 13 July , he scored the first in a 3–0 win over Zaria Bălți in the UEFA Europa League second qualifying round . He scored his second goal in as many games in the second leg match played the following week , as Apollon made it through to the third qualifying round .

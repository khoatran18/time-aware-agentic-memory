 Miller M . Duris ( March 2 , 1928 – March 23 , 2015 ) was an American politician in the state of Oregon . A native Oregonian , he served as mayor of Hillsboro , Oregon , and as chairman of the Washington County Board of Commissioners in the 1970s and 1980s . He served in the United States Navy and worked for nearly 30 years at Tektronix .

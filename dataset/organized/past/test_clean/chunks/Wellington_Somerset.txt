Wellington_Somerset | History
Arthur Wellesley took the title of his Marquessate in 1809 from this town of Wellington . Nearby Wellington Hill boasts a large , spotlit obelisk to his honour . The Wellington Monument is a floodlit high triangular tower designated by English Heritage as a grade II* listed building . It was erected to celebrate the Duke of Wellingtons victory at the Battle of Waterloo .

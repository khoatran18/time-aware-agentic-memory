Minna_Canth | Minna Canth
 Minna Canth was the first major Finnish-language playwright and prose writer after Aleksis Kivi , the national author of Finland , and the first Finnish-language newspaper woman . She was also the first woman to receive her own flag day in Finland , starting on 19 March 2009 . It is also the day of social equality in Finland .

 Life after active duty . Since December 1941 , U-38 has been used as a training boat in the 24th and 21st U-boat Flotillas . She was then used as a testing boat , until she was scuttled by her crew on 5 May 1945 .

=== CHUNK /wiki/German_submarine_U-38_(1938)#15@past ===
German_submarine_U_38_1938 | Service history
After completing the clandestine reconnaissance mission , U-38 returned to raiding duties and sank three ships , two British and one Greek . The British steam freighter SS Thomas Walton was sunk on 7 December . The Greek steam freighter SS Garoufalia was sunk on 11 December , and so was the British steam freighter on 13 December . After an operational period of four and a half weeks , U-38 returned to Wilhelmshaven on 16 December .

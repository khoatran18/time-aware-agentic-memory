John_Conyers | John Conyers
 After his active military service , Conyers pursued a college education . He earned both his BA ( 1957 ) and LL.B . ( 1958 ) degrees from Wayne State University . After he was admitted to the bar , he worked on the staff of Congressman John Dingell . He also served as counsel to several Detroit-area labor union locals . From 1961 to 1963 , he was a referee for Ohios workmens compensation department .

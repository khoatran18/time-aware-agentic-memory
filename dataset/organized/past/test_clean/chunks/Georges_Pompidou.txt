Georges_Pompidou | President
After the failure of the 1969 constitutional referendum , de Gaulle resigned and Pompidou was elected president of France . During the 15 June 1969 general election , he beat the centrist President of the Senate and Acting President Alain Poher by a large margin ( 57%–42% ) . Though a Gaullist , Pompidou was more pragmatic than de Gaulle , notably facilitating the accession of the United Kingdom to the European Community on 1 January 1973 .

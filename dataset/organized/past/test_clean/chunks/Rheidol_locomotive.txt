Rheidol , formerly named Treze de Maio and Talybont , was a steam locomotive built by W.G . Bagnall in Staffordshire , England , in 1896 . Originally built to a gauge of , it was ordered to work at a Brazilian sugar plantation , however the order was cancelled before it was exported . The locomotive was then regauged and renamed to work on the -gauge Plynlimon and Hafan Tramway , in Wales , where the locomotive worked from August 1898 until 1899 .
